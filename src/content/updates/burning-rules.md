This is a plain-language summary of public guidance. It is not the TCEQ rule and it is not a county order.

When no burn ban is in effect, a campfire, cooking fire, or brush pile can still be illegal if it breaks the state’s outdoor-burning rule. ESD No. 2 tells residents to burn natural material from that property, stay well away from buildings, keep a way to put the fire out, and have it cold before sunset. Trash, tires, treated lumber, shingles, and chemicals are not yard waste.

If a neighbor’s smoke becomes a complaint, the fire can be ordered out. If it escapes, call 911.

Read the [county burning page](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/page/em.outdoor_burning) and the [ESD No. 2 notes](https://www.bastropesd2.org/outdoor-burning/) before you start.
