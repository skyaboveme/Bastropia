This is a summary of the public alert program, not a message from dispatch.

Bastrop County’s emergency management office coordinates disaster notices with cities and fire districts. Countywide alerts go through Warn Central Texas. Cell phones have to be registered. You can add more than one address.

The county has said it does not take emergency requests through social media. If you need help now, call 911.

[Create a Warn Central Texas account](https://warncentraltexas.org/).
