This is a summary of the county’s meeting order for fiscal year 2026–2027.

Regular terms are the second and fourth Monday of the month, at the county seat, in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor of the annex at 804 Pecan Street. A county holiday can move the day. Tuesday, October 13, 2026 was that kind of exception. The weekday below is the weekday of the date the order names.
