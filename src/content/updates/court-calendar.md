This is a summary of the county’s meeting order for fiscal year 2025–2026.

Regular terms are the second and fourth Monday of the month, at the county seat, in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor of the annex at 804 Pecan Street. The order lists exceptions when a county holiday moves the day.

Dates still ahead from that order:

- Monday, October 27, 2026
- Monday, November 10, 2026
- Monday, November 24, 2026
- Monday, December 8, 2026
- Monday, December 22, 2026

The order does not print a start time. Use the agenda on the county website.
