This is a summary of a public county page, not a new order from the county.

As of October 27, 2026, the Bastrop County outdoor burning page says there is no burn ban in effect. The same page keeps earlier orders, including September 28, September 14, and August 24, 2026.

Check that page the day you plan to burn. Bastropia copies the status into the top of this site, and a person still has to update it when the county changes the order.
