Smithville sits at the east end of the county, with a Main Street city hall, a river park the city actually runs, and a state park that uses a Smithville mailing address. Those are three different operators. This guide keeps them straight and leaves out any festival lineup that was not on a city or state page.

## City hall and the council

The City of Smithville lists its offices at 317 Main Street, P.O. Box 449, Smithville, TX 78957. The phone is 512-237-3282. City offices are open 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday.

The council page says Smithville uses a council-manager government. The council adopts ordinances and resolutions. The city manager carries out the policy and runs city operations. The council is the mayor and five members. They are elected at large, which means each of them represents the whole city and every registered city voter may vote in all six places. When this guide was updated, the mayor listed on that page was Sharon Foerster. The staff directory listed Trey Job as city manager. The council page says questions can go to City Secretary Jennifer Lynch at 512-237-3282, extension 2106.

The overview on the council page does not lock meetings to a single weekday and time. Agendas and packets are in the Agenda Center. The city calendar lists a meeting when it is set. One notice, for November 9, 2026, says the council will meet at 6:00 p.m. at City Hall, 317 Main Street, under the Texas Open Meetings Act. The Agenda Center for 2026 also holds a long run of special called meetings with their own times. Treat the current PDF as the appointment. A remembered “second Tuesday” will be wrong often enough to matter.

The [government guide](/guides/bastrop-county-government) compares that habit with Bastrop, which does print “second and fourth Tuesday at 6:30 p.m.,” and Elgin, which prints “first and third Tuesday at 6:30 p.m.” Smithville is the one of the three that asks you to open the notice.

The [Smithville town page](/smithville) has the week’s weather, Smithville ISD dates that are posted, and local listings. County court, if you need it, is still in Bastrop at 804 Pecan Street. The [court page](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/page/co.com_court) has the 9 a.m. dates.

## Parks, the river, and Buescher

The city parks page lists Independence Park, Keilburg Park, Main Street Park, MLK Park, Railroad Park, Riverbend Park, a skate park, and the Veterans Memorial and Splash Park. It says all Smithville parks are closed between 11:30 p.m. and 6 a.m. Questions go to parks and recreation at 512-237-3282, extension 7.

The recreation center is at 106 Royston Street. The parks and recreation page lists hours of Monday through Thursday 8 a.m. to 9 p.m., Friday 8 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., and Saturday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., closed Sunday. The same department says Riverbend Park and the recreation center have had Wi-Fi, and it notes that Wi-Fi is currently unavailable at Riverbend. That parenthetical is on the city page. Do not plan a trip around a connection there until the city takes the note down.

Riverbend is the city’s campground on the Colorado River. The camping page says sites include electricity at 30 and 50 amp, and water. A main dump station is available. The grounds are toward the back of the park near the river. Campers can use a restroom building with air conditioning and showers. You need a camping permit. Reservations are made ahead by email or by calling the recreation center Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., at 512-237-3282, extension 7. The page says after-hours or weekend arrivals that were not reserved are limited and cash only.

The posted rate table, on that same page, lists $20 a site per day, $100 a site per week, and $350 for a month with a 30-day maximum. Special events and city holiday weekends are listed at $25 a site per day, and the weekly rate is not offered in those holiday periods. A site holds a maximum of one RV or two tents. Those numbers are what the city page showed when this guide was updated. If you are booking for a holiday, read the table again. The page defines a holiday rate as a city-observed holiday that falls on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or Monday, charged through that weekend.

Buescher State Park is not a city park. Texas Parks & Wildlife lists it at 100 Park Road 1E, Smithville, TX 78957, phone (512) 237-2241, open daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. The park page describes a 30-acre lake, nearly six miles of trail, and loaner fishing gear with no license required on that lake. Adult day use is listed at $5, and children 12 and under are free. The Civilian Conservation Corps built the lake, the dam, and the group buildings in the 1930s. Park Road 1C is the 12-mile connection west to Bastrop State Park. The longer park note is in the [things-to-do guide](/guides/things-to-do-in-bastrop-county).

Visit Bastrop’s visitor guide also mentions a boat ramp in Smithville under the highway bridge at Highway 95, and Vernon L. Richards Riverbend Park, as river access. The city’s own park name on the camping page is Riverbend Park. Use the city page to reserve a site. Use TPWD for Buescher.

## Schools and the county tax counter

Smithville ISD’s homepage lists P.O. Box 479, Smithville, TX 78957, phone (512) 237-2487, and the email sisdcommunications@smithvilleisd.org. The [school-status page](/schools) reads that homepage for a closing or a delay dated today. The district calendar, not this guide, has the holiday list.

The county tax substation in Smithville is at 1624 NE Loop 230, Smithville, TX 78957, (512) 581-7114. The county says that starting Monday, October 5, the substation is open only on Tuesdays and Fridays until further notice, from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed 12 to 1. No title transfers after 4 p.m. The main tax office, if Tuesday and Friday do not work, is 211 Jackson Street in Bastrop, (512) 581-7161, Monday through Friday. Homestead paperwork goes to the appraisal district. The [moving guide](/guides/moving-to-bastrop-county) has the January 31 due date and the April 30 homestead deadline from the Texas Comptroller.

The burn ban is a county order even in Smithville. Check the [burn-ban page](/burn-ban) and the [fire-safety guide](/guides/burn-bans-and-fire-safety). A campfire ring at Buescher is still subject to a county ban, and a debris fire inside the city has to meet both the county order and the state’s city-limits rule.

The [phone list](/phone-numbers) has the Smithville police number and the library hours that were checked against the library’s own page. This guide does not repeat a library schedule it did not reopen. For this week’s events, use the [town page](/smithville) and the [calendar](/events).
