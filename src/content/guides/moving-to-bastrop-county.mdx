People moving in usually need the same short list: which town government, if any, covers the house, which school district the address sits in, who sells the electricity and the water, and which county office sends the tax bill. The answers are on public pages. This guide points at those pages and skips anything a city or the county did not post.

## The towns

Bastrop, Elgin, and Smithville each publish a city website and a city hall number. Bastrop’s city line is (512) 332-8800. Elgin’s city page lists 310 N. Main Street and (512) 281-5724. Smithville’s offices are at 317 Main Street, open 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday, at (512) 237-3282.

Paige is different. The Handbook of Texas lists it as an unincorporated town in northeast Bastrop County, on U.S. 290, about 44 miles east of Austin, with State Highway 21 along the north side. It has no city council. County government is the local government there. Cedar Creek and McDade are on this site as their own town pages. Use the town page for the week’s weather and listings, and use the county for roads, taxes, and the burn ban outside a city.

The county seat is Bastrop. The courthouse address printed on the county site is 804 Pecan Street, Bastrop, TX 78602, and the main county phone on that site is (512) 581-7100. County Judge Gregory Klaus’s office is at that courthouse, (512) 332-7201. The judge’s page lists hours of 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Thursday and 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Friday, closed 12 to 1 for lunch.

Commissioners Court is the county judge plus four precinct commissioners. The court meets in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor of the annex at 804 Pecan Street. The [government guide](/guides/bastrop-county-government) explains how to read a meeting date. The [new-resident page](/new-here) names the current commissioners and links the precinct map, which is the way to learn who covers your road.

## Electric and water

Do not open a utility account from the town name alone.

Bluebonnet Electric Cooperative’s start-service page says to check the co-op’s service-area map. Where Bluebonnet is the provider, the same page says member services is 800-842-7708 and the outage line is 800-949-4414. Have the service address, and the meter number, pole number, or physical address, before you call. The stop-service page says member services answers 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday.

Aqua Water Supply Corporation’s contact page lists 415 Old Austin Highway, Drawer P, Bastrop, TX 78602, and phone 512-303-3943 for customer service and for an emergency leak. The lobby is open 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed 12 to 1. The drive-through is 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., also closed 12 to 1. If your address is not on Aqua’s system, that number will not start city water.

Inside Bastrop, Elgin, or Smithville, start with that city’s utility desk. City water, city trash, and a rural co-op are different accounts. The [phone-number list](/phone-numbers) links the city utility and trash pages that were on file for this desk. The [new-resident page](/new-here) keeps the trash-day notes for the three cities. Paige is not one of those cities, so a Paige address is not on the Bastrop, Elgin, or Smithville city trash calendars.

## School districts

Four districts publish homepages that this site watches for closures: Bastrop ISD, Elgin ISD, Smithville ISD, and McDade ISD. The [school-status page](/schools) says whether a district has posted a closing or a delay today. A normal day there means the homepage had no closing dated today. It is not a promise about next week.

Bastrop ISD’s about page says the district serves more than 13,000 students in Bastrop, Cedar Creek, Red Rock, Rockne, and Paige, and that it covers 433 square miles. The district office on that page is 906 Farm Street, Bastrop, TX 78602, (512) 772-7100. The district also publishes attendance-zone maps. The page reviewed for this guide included maps for the 2026–2027 school year. Use the map for the address, not the neighborhood nickname.

Elgin ISD’s about page says the district is in Elgin and spans 168 square miles across portions of Bastrop, Lee, and Travis counties, with more than 6,000 students on 10 campuses. It lists the district as founded in 1897, with the Wildcats as the mascot. The contact block is P.O. Box 351, 1002 N. Avenue C, Elgin, TX 78621, (512) 281-3434. The about page names Dr. Christopher Bailey as superintendent.

Smithville ISD’s homepage lists P.O. Box 479, Smithville, TX 78957, and (512) 237-2487. McDade ISD’s homepage lists P.O. Box 400, 156 Marlin Street, McDade, TX 78650, and 512-273-2522.

A Paige mailing address is not, by itself, a school assignment. Bastrop ISD’s about page includes Paige among the communities it serves. Rural lines still move. Open the zone map, or call the district, before you register a student.

## Property tax basics

Texas property tax is local. The Texas Comptroller’s “Paying Your Taxes” page says taxing units start mailing bills in October and that payment is due upon receipt. In most cases the tax must be paid by January 31. Tax that is still unpaid on February 1 is delinquent, and penalty and interest are added. The Comptroller’s page says that on February 1 the penalty is 6 percent and the interest is 1 percent, that the penalty keeps rising by 1 percent a month until July 1, when the penalty becomes 12 percent, and that interest continues at 1 percent a month with no stated maximum.

There is a timing exception. If the original bill is mailed after January 10, the delinquency date is postponed so you have at least 21 days to pay. The Comptroller says that postponed date is printed on the bill. Not receiving a bill does not, by itself, erase the tax.

The bill in this county comes from the Bastrop County Tax Assessor-Collector. The county’s locations page names Ellen Owens in that office. The main office is 211 Jackson Street, Bastrop, TX 78602, (512) 581-7161, open Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a drive-through from 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. The same page lists substations in Cedar Creek at 5785 FM 535, (512) 581-4270; in Elgin at 1125 Dildy Drive, (512) 581-7146; and in Smithville at 1624 NE Loop 230, (512) 581-7114. Smithville’s substation, starting Monday, October 5, is open Tuesdays and Fridays only until further notice, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed 12 to 1. None of those four counters is in Paige.

Value and exemptions are a different office. The Bastrop Central Appraisal District handles appraisal and exemptions. Its site is bastropcad.org, and the phone published with the district in the county’s own new-resident notes is (512) 303-1930. The Comptroller’s homestead guide says you file the residence homestead application with the appraisal district in the county where the property sits, not with the Comptroller. The usual deadline is April 30. A late application for a general residence homestead, including age 65 or older or disabled, may be filed up to two years after that deadline. This guide does not quote a local tax rate. Rates are set by the taxing units on your bill, and they are not one number for the whole county.

## First-week offices

Register for Warn Central Texas. The county Office of Emergency Management says the Capital Area Council of Governments system can call, email, or text during a disaster or public-safety event, and that you choose the time and method. The OEM office is at 1501 Business Park Drive, Bastrop, (512) 581-4022. Its mailing address is the courthouse, 804 Pecan Street.

Bookmark the [burn-ban page](/burn-ban). The county posts the order on its outdoor-burning page, and this site repeats that status. The [fire-safety guide](/guides/burn-bans-and-fire-safety) explains why a day with no ban is still not a free pass to burn.

For a road, a precinct question, or a meeting, use the [government guide](/guides/bastrop-county-government) and the [phone list](/phone-numbers). Sheriff, clerk, and court numbers live on those pages with the county links beside them. This guide does not retype every desk.
