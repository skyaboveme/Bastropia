Elgin is one of the three incorporated cities in Bastrop County, and it does not behave like a neighborhood of Bastrop. It has its own council, its own school district, and a downtown festival the city itself produces. County government still matters here for the tax bill, the burn ban, and anything outside the city limits. This guide keeps those two layers separate.

## City hall and the council

The City of Elgin lists city hall at 310 N. Main Street, P.O. Box 591, Elgin, TX 78621. The phone on the city’s contact block is 512-281-5724. The fax on that block is 512-285-3016.

The council page says meetings are the first and third Tuesday of each month at 6:30 p.m. at the Library Annex. It also says the time and date are subject to change, that additional meetings may be set with three business days’ notice, and that agendas are posted three business days before the meeting. Minutes are posted after the council approves them. If you only remember “Tuesday night,” you will miss a moved meeting. Open the agenda.

The page lists a mayor at large and two members for each of four wards. When this guide was updated, the mayor at large was Theresa Y. McShan, with a term ending May 2028. The page also names a mayor pro tem and a deputy mayor pro tem among the ward members. Those names and the term years are on the council page. They are the roster to trust, not a reprint that will go stale at the next election.

People who need help to attend, under the Americans with Disabilities Act, are asked to call the city secretary at 512-229-3222. The page asks for 48 hours’ notice when that is feasible, and it says the council chambers are wheelchair accessible with marked parking in front of the building.

The council page says you can watch past city council meetings and city videos from that page. Use that link rather than a third-party clip. The [government guide](/guides/bastrop-county-government) puts Elgin’s meeting next to Bastrop’s and Smithville’s so the three calendars are easy to tell apart. Bastrop meets on the second and fourth Tuesday. Elgin meets on the first and third. They are not the same night.

The [Elgin town page](/elgin) is the weekly view: weather, Elgin ISD dates that are on the calendar, and listings. A desk story on the city’s mixed-drink election is [here](/news/elgin-voters-approve-citywide-mixed-drink-sales). Another local note is the [Operation Turkey meal count](/news/operation-turkey-elgin-3200-thanksgiving-meals-2026). Both are news, with their own sources, not standing city policy.

## Elgin ISD

Elgin ISD’s about page describes a district based in Elgin, 25 miles east of Austin, covering 168 square miles in portions of Bastrop, Lee, and Travis counties. It says the district serves more than 6,000 students on 10 campuses, from early childhood through 12th grade, and that it was founded in 1897. The mascot is the Wildcats. The colors listed are purple and white. The about page names Dr. Christopher Bailey as superintendent.

The district office block is P.O. Box 351, 1002 N. Avenue C, Elgin, TX 78621, phone 512-281-3434. Closings and delays, when the district posts them on the homepage, show up on the [school-status page](/schools). A “normal day” there means no closing was posted for today. It does not describe the rest of the semester.

Because the district crosses into Lee and Travis counties, a friend in Elgin and a friend just outside it can be in different systems, or the same system, depending on the map. Bastrop ISD’s about page does not list Elgin among its communities. If you are between the two, use the address, not the grocery store you use.

## Hogeye and downtown

The city’s Hogeye page is the authoritative one. It says the festival is a downtown street festival on the fourth Saturday of October, brought by the Elgin Main Street Program. It says the city has been a Texas Main Street City since 1990. The festival is described as a celebration of the community and of Elgin sausage, produced by the Main Street board and local volunteers, and underwritten by sponsors. The page says the Saturday festival is free.

The 2026 page calls it the 39th annual Hogeye and lists three days: Thursday, October 22, Friday, October 23, and Saturday, October 24. Saturday hours are 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. The page describes a pork cook-off, live music on two stages, a children’s area, and a free shuttle from SPJST Hall No. 18 at 702 N. Highway 95. Thursday and Friday have their own events on that page, including an art show and a street dance. Those details are for 2026. Next year’s city page is the one to reread. The pattern to remember is the fourth Saturday in October, downtown, free to walk in.

This guide does not list sausage makers or restaurants. The city page is proud of the sausage. The shops are the city’s and the chamber’s to name.

## County services that still apply

Living in Elgin does not take you out of Bastrop County. The county tax office has a substation at 1125 Dildy Drive, Elgin, TX 78621, (512) 581-7146, open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The county says it does not do title transfers after 4 p.m. The bill is the county’s. A homestead exemption is filed with the Bastrop Central Appraisal District, not at the Elgin counter. The deadline and the two-office split are in the [moving guide](/guides/moving-to-bastrop-county).

Commissioners Court still meets in Bastrop, at 804 Pecan Street, on the dates the county posts, generally at 9 a.m. A city ordinance is the council’s. A county burn ban is the court’s. The [burn-ban page](/burn-ban) repeats the county order. Inside the city, the Texas outdoor-burning rule also says burning has to be outside city limits unless the city has an ordinance that allows it. Read the [fire-safety guide](/guides/burn-bans-and-fire-safety) before you treat a quiet weekend as permission.

Elgin’s police department is the city’s. The number this site carries, from the city’s own materials, is on the [phone list](/phone-numbers). For an emergency, call 911.

Trash and recycling follow the city’s calendar, not a county route. The [new-resident page](/new-here) links the city trash calendar that was on file. Electric and water depend on whether the address is on a city utility or on Bluebonnet or Aqua. Check the provider map, or the city utility desk, before you open an account in the wrong system. Those contacts are in the moving guide, with the hours copied from Bluebonnet and Aqua.

Elgin is close enough to Austin that people treat it as a commute town, and local enough that the festival still shuts down downtown once a year. The pages above are the ones the city and the district actually maintain. Start there, and use the town page when you only need this week.
