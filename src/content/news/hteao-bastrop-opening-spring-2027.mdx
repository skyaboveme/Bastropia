If you've noticed construction on SH 71 West and wondered when the tea shop is coming, here's the latest. Bastrop's first HTeaO is taking shape at 532 SH 71 W. at The Grove, but it won't open until next spring.

[Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/dining/hteao-takes-shape-ahead-of-bastrop-debut/) reported Nov. 14 that construction delays have pushed the drink stop well past its original schedule. Work had been expected to run from October 2025 to March 2026, with an opening in mid- to late 2026. Company officials now told the publication that the shop is on track to open in late spring 2027, in the second quarter of the year.

The building is about 2,078 square feet. Community Impact previously reported (August 2025) that it's estimated to cost around $1 million.

**What it will serve**

HTeaO is a Texas-based chain known for freshly brewed iced tea, both sweet and unsweet, in more than two dozen flavors. Community Impact lists options like Georgia peach, watermelon and wild raspberry. The menu also includes hot and iced coffee, lemonade and fruit-flavored energy drinks, plus a rotating "secret menu" of seasonal and limited-time flavors.

**For drivers**

The site is on one of Bastrop's busiest corridors. There's no opening date or traffic plan yet, so expect ongoing construction activity near The Grove through the winter. Bastropia will share an opening date when the company announces one. More about the chain is at [hteao.com](https://www.hteao.com).
