This week's Eats Beat is a countywide look at happy hours and specials posted by restaurants and bars in Bastrop, Elgin and Smithville. The listings below come from each venue's own website or official specials page, checked Sunday, Nov. 15, and Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. A few places also name promotions without posting the day, time or price; those are noted as such.

Specials change, sometimes from one week to the next. Please confirm the deal, the price and the hours with the restaurant or bar before you go. If you run a venue in Bastrop County and have a current happy hour or special, send the details through the [Bastropia Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bastropia).
