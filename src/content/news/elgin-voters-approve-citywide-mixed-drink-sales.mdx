The result of Elgin's Nov. 3 alcohol election is now official. On Tuesday night, Nov. 17, the Elgin City Council approved a resolution canvassing the special local option election, the city announced on its News Flash page on Nov. 18.

**What voters decided**

The ballot asked whether alcoholic beverages, including mixed drinks, may be legally sold everywhere inside Elgin's current city limits. According to the city, the measure passed with **77.6%** of the vote. A total of **3,347 ballots** were cast, with election officials in both Bastrop and Travis counties running the election, since Elgin sits in both.

**Why it was on the ballot**

Until now, mixed drinks could be sold in only part of Elgin. The city says the dividing line was the area inside city limits in 1995, the year of Elgin's last alcohol election. Neighborhoods and commercial areas annexed since then were outside that zone. The new vote applies to the city as it exists today.

Restaurants and bars that want to serve mixed drinks still need the appropriate state permits from the Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission, so changes on the ground may take time.

**New Ward 1 council member**

At the same meeting, **Jessica Jaimes** took the oath of office as the Ward 1 council member. The city said she ran unopposed in the Nov. 3 special election to fill a vacancy. Municipal Court Judge Amanda Carter swore her in, and her first term runs through May 2028.

**Nonprofit funding committee**

The council also named the members who will review this year's requests from nonprofits that serve Elgin: Deputy Mayor Pro Tem Tiffany St. Pierre and council members Sue Brashar, Agatha Mayfield and Roland Silva. The city says it has set aside about **$50,000** a year for nonprofits in recent years, but it received nearly **$150,000** in requests for this fiscal year. The full council will vote later on the committee's recommendations.

The council also adopted an official investment policy. Under it, a designated investment officer will give the council regular reports, as the state's Public Funds Investment Act requires.

Elgin's next regular City Council meeting is set for Dec. 1. Agendas are posted on the city's CivicClerk portal at https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/.
