Bastrop's only Whataburger is set to reopen Tuesday, Dec. 8, at 401 SH 71 W., according to [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/dining/whataburger-to-make-its-bastrop-comeback-dec-8/). The reopening comes nearly five months after the restaurant closed for a rebuild.

**What happened**

The original building, which had stood for about 25 years, was torn down in July. Community Impact reported that the $2.5 million project replaces it with a new restaurant of about 3,800 square feet.

**What's different**

According to Community Impact, the new building includes:

- **Two drive-thru lanes**
- **Curbside pickup** for orders placed online or in the app
- **Updated digital menu boards**
- **New kitchen equipment and technology**

Operating partner Amy Zamora told Community Impact the design is meant to make ordering easier and to help the location's 40 employees get orders out more efficiently.

**Good to know**

- **Opening date:** Tuesday, Dec. 8 (hours weren't included in the report)
- **Address:** 401 SH 71 W., Bastrop
- **Store page:** [locations.whataburger.com/tx/bastrop/401-tx-71.html](https://locations.whataburger.com/tx/bastrop/401-tx-71.html)

The restaurant sits on one of the county's busiest stretches of SH 71. If you're driving past on opening week, expect more traffic turning in and out of the lot, especially at lunch and dinner. Opening dates can shift, so check the store page before you go.
