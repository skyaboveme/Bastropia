Friday, October 30 is the last day of early voting. The county schedule lists polls open from 7 am to 7 pm. A voter can use any early voting location: the courthouse annex at 804 Pecan Street in Bastrop, the Smithville Recreation Center, the Cedar Creek annex, or the North Bastrop County Community Annex in Elgin.

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, with polls open from 7 am to 7 pm. Community Impact reports that CARTS will offer free rides to and from polling places in Bastrop County that day. The code is VoteTexas2026 in the CARTS Country Bus or CARTS NOW app. Riders can also call [512-478-7433](tel:5124787433).

[See hours and polling places](/go-vote). The schedule is on [bastropvotes.org](https://www.bastropvotes.org/voting-schedule/). The ride offer is in [Community Impact’s report](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/election/carts-to-offer-free-election-day-rides/).
