Thanksgiving weekend is packed with things to do close to home. Here is what's on the calendar for Friday through Sunday, plus a look at next week.

**Friday, Nov. 27**

- **Bastrop tree lighting and River of Lights:** 6 to 8 p.m. at Fisherman's Park, free. River of Lights then opens nightly, 6 to 10 p.m., through Jan. 4. Full details: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-tree-lighting-river-of-lights-friday-nov-27-2026

**Saturday, Nov. 28**

- **Rockne Market Day:** The 10th annual market runs **9 a.m. to 4 p.m.** at **4045 FM 535** in Rockne. Organizers' listing says vendors will fill both Holtman Hall and Sacred Heart Hall with handmade goods, canned goods, woodworking, clothing and holiday items. Listing: https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/rockne-market-day-2026-2026-11-28
- **New museum exhibit:** The Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center, 904 Main St., opens **"Holiday Traditions from Around the World"** at 10 a.m. The exhibit looks at how different cultures and faiths celebrate holidays like Christmas, Hanukkah and Kwanzaa, and visitors are invited to share their own family traditions. Its grand opening is during Wassail Fest, and it runs through **Dec. 31**. Museum hours are 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Saturday and 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Sunday. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/holiday-traditions-from-around-the-world-2026-11-28
- **Small Business Saturday:** Wassail Fest fills downtown Bastrop, and downtown Elgin hosts Shop Small Saturday. Times and details: https://bastropia.com/news/small-business-saturday-2026-wassail-fest-bastrop-shop-small-elgin

**Sunday, Nov. 29: First Sunday of Advent**

Many churches mark the start of Advent this Sunday. The Bastrop County Calendar lists Sunday worship times for dozens of congregations across the county: https://bastropcountycalendar.com/

- **The Pentecostals of Bastrop**, 261 FM 20, hold Sunday school at 10 a.m. and worship at **11 a.m.** The service is also livestreamed; recent messages are on the church's YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCsh-GCAlLiVJs7E8Ags1UDg
- **Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Rockne** lists a Saturday vigil Mass at 5 p.m. and Sunday Masses at 8 a.m. and 10:30 a.m. (Spanish).

**Weekend weather**

Saturday looks sunny with a high near **73**, but gusts could reach 35 mph. A dry front Sunday brings a high near 66 and gusty northwest wind. Our full forecast: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-forecast-freeze-warning-warm-windy-weekend

**Looking ahead**

- **Tuesday, Dec. 1:** Elgin's EDC board meets at 5 p.m. and City Council at 6:30 p.m. Agendas: https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/
- **Thursday, Dec. 3:** Bastrop's Old Iron Bridge is scheduled to reopen. Elgin's Library Advisory Board meets at 5:30 p.m. to discuss its 2027 budget.
- **Saturday, Dec. 5:** St. Peter's Lutheran Church plans a live nativity in Elgin's Veterans Memorial Park.
