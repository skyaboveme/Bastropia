If a fresh-cut tree is part of your holiday plans, you don't have to drive far. Community Impact rounded up three choose-and-cut farms near Bastrop on Nov. 19, and the first one opens this weekend. The outlet notes its list is not comprehensive.

**Elgin Christmas Tree Farm**

- **Where:** 120 Natures Way, Elgin
- **Opens:** Saturday, Nov. 21, through Dec. 20 or until trees sell out
- **Hours:** Weekdays noon–5 p.m.; weekend hours vary
- **Trees:** Choose-and-cut pine and cypress, plus precut Fraser firs
- **Prices:** $59–$289
- **Extras:** Saws, twine, tree shaking and baling at no extra charge
- **Website:** https://www.elginchristmastreefarm.com

**Evergreen Farms Christmas Trees**

- **Where:** 242 Monkey Road, Elgin
- **Opens:** Friday, Nov. 27, until sold out
- **Hours:** Thursdays through Sundays; times vary
- **Trees:** Choose-and-cut pine and cypress, plus precut Fraser and noble firs
- **Prices:** $48–$220
- **Extras:** Saws, twine, tree shaking and netting at no extra charge
- **Website:** https://www.evergreenfarms.biz

**Lee County Christmas Trees**

- **Where:** 4067 County Road 114, Lincoln (in neighboring Lee County)
- **Opens:** Friday, Nov. 27, until sold out
- **Hours:** Weekends; times vary
- **Trees:** Choose-and-cut Virginia pine and Murray cypress
- **Prices:** Vary
- **Extras:** Staff can help load trees onto vehicles
- **Website:** https://www.leecountychristmastrees.com

**Before you go**

Hours at all three farms change from day to day and supplies can run out, so check each farm's website before you head out, especially on weekends. Measure your ceiling height and stand before you pick a tree. Bring gloves and something to tie the tree down if your vehicle doesn't have a roof rack. Once you're home, a fresh cut tree stays green longest when its stand is kept full of water.
