Bastrop's holiday season officially kicks off Friday night, the day after Thanksgiving, with the city's Christmas tree lighting at Fisherman's Park.

**When and where**

- **Friday, Nov. 27, 6 to 8 p.m.**
- **Fisherman's Park, 1200 Willow St., Bastrop**
- **Free** admission

**What to expect**

Bastrop ISD student choirs will perform first. Then Santa is expected to arrive on a Bastrop fire truck before the tree is lit, according to Discover Bastrop, as reported by Community Impact.

**River of Lights opens the same night**

After the ceremony, the River of Lights opens for the season. It stretches about **a half mile** along the June Hill Pape Riverwalk, with hundreds of holiday displays. The path runs below Bastrop's Old Iron Bridge, which we reported last week is scheduled to reopen Dec. 3 after an $11 million renovation.

River of Lights is open **every night from 6 to 10 p.m. through Jan. 4**, so there is plenty of time to go if Friday is busy.

**Dress for a chilly night**

The National Weather Service expects a mostly clear Friday night with a low around **40**. Bring a jacket, and maybe a blanket for little ones.

**More Santa time this weekend**

The Hyatt Regency Lost Pines Resort lists several holiday events starting Friday, including free photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus in the lobby from 6:15 to 8:15 p.m. Friday (reservations requested) and a Santa's Workshop open daily from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. through Dec. 24. Check the resort's listings for details and any fees: https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/photos-with-santa-2026-11-27
