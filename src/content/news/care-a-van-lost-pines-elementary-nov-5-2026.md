Families looking for an evening of play and a chance to ask about community resources have a local option this Thursday. Bastrop County Cares lists a Care-A-Van visit to Lost Pines Elementary on Nov. 5, from 5 to 7 p.m. The event location is 151 Tiger Woods Drive in Bastrop.

The nonprofit describes Care-A-Van as a way to bring recreation and family connections into neighborhoods, including rural areas where access to parks and playgrounds may be limited. Its event description emphasizes positive parent-child interaction alongside information about local resources.

The program’s listed recreational activities include soccer, basketball, archery and tag, as well as reading tents and creative play. These are activities the organizer describes for Care-A-Van, not a guaranteed lineup for every stop. Some activities depend on the weather, and the organization says schedules may change as needed. Check the event page before heading out.

While children take part in activities, parents and caregivers can connect with the nonprofit’s Parent Ambassadors and Community Health Workers. The event description says those conversations can help families learn about local programs and support, making the gathering more than a recreation stop.

Topics listed by the organizer include public-benefit enrollment, such as SNAP, WIC and Medicaid, along with early-childhood services and home-visiting programs. It also names workforce-development programs, digital-literacy opportunities and basic-needs assistance. Families can use the visit to ask which resources might fit their circumstances and what steps to take next.

Those resource connections should not be confused with a promise of immediate enrollment, benefit approval or a particular service on site. The organizer describes an opportunity to learn about programs and support. Anyone who needs a specific kind of assistance should contact Bastrop County Cares ahead of time to ask what will be available at this stop.

The Lost Pines Elementary visit is listed as a recurring first-Thursday gathering, but this notice is specifically for Nov. 5. The published window is two hours, beginning at 5 p.m. For families planning around the evening, the key details are the school venue, the 5–7 p.m. schedule and the weather caveat—not the nonprofit’s separate office address.

For questions or the latest event information, Bastrop County Cares lists 512-409-3116 and [Communications@bastropcares.org](mailto:Communications@bastropcares.org) on its contact page. Review the [Nov. 5 event notice](https://www.bastropcares.org/community-event/care-a-van-event-blessed-hope-ministries-6/2026-11-05/) before leaving, especially if your family is counting on a particular activity or resource conversation.
