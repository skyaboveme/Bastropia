Bastrop County Commissioners Court has posted its agenda for its next regular session. It meets **Monday, Nov. 23, at 9 a.m.** in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor of 804 Pecan St. in Bastrop. Here are the items most likely to matter to residents, based on the posted agenda.

**Taxes and budget**

- **2026 certified tax levy** for Bastrop County, and the certified levy for the county's Farm-to-Market Lateral Road and Bridge District 1.
- **Certified 2026 tax rolls** for Double Eagle Ranch Public Improvement Districts 1 and 2.
- **Double Eagle Ranch PID, Improvement Area 3:** a resolution on the cost of planned improvements, a preliminary assessment plan and assessment roll, and a call for a **public hearing on Dec. 14** about levying those assessments. A second item would approve a preliminary offering document for 2027 special assessment revenue bonds for the same area.
- A position reclassification, salary adjustment and budget amendment for the County Judge's office.

**Data center discussion**

Commissioner Carmack placed a **discussion-only** item on EdgeConnex, including possible tax abatements and the community benefits the company is proposing. Discussion-only items are not up for a vote at this meeting.

**Roads, drainage and development**

- Accepting the Los Milagros Phase 5 and 6 roads into the county road system (Centella Circle, Crisanto Drive, Peonia Pass, Los Milagros Drive, Amaranta Lane, Pajarilla Pass and Gisofila Lane, Precinct 3), and releasing the developer's two-year maintenance bond.
- Six-month extensions for Los Milagros Phase 7 (66 lots) and Phase 8 (47 lots).
- The Terra Acres subdivision: 16 lots on about 18.9 acres in Precinct 3.
- A contract with Mills Construction for **Cardinal Drive drainage improvements**, funded through the state's GLO CDBG mitigation program.
- Setting a Dec. 28 public hearing on a Hidden Oaks Section 2 replat in Precinct 4.

**Safety and health**

The court is set to accept a FEMA hazard mitigation award for phase 1 of a **fuels reduction project on lands next to Bastrop State Park**. The Public Health Department is scheduled to report on infectious diseases and give an update on the Grady Tuck Clinic. The consent agenda includes a state rural sheriff's office salary grant and new county CERT volunteer policies. Commissioners will also discuss a possible purchase of about 10 acres on Lovers Lane.

**How to speak**

Fill out the county's Public Participation Form and hand it to the County Clerk before the court reaches your item. Speakers usually get three minutes. Topics that aren't on the agenda can be raised during Citizen Comments near the start of the meeting, but the court can't discuss them then.
