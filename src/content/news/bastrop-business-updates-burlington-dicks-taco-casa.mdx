Bastrop County's business scene has been busy this fall. Here's a roundup of openings, projects on the way and one closing, based on [Community Impact's Nov. 12 report](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/business/10-bastrop-business-updates-to-know/).

**Recently opened**

- **Mr. Brew**, 49 Loop 150 W., Bastrop. Owner Jaime Astorga brought the coffee shop back in the former Coffee Dog Roastery space, serving coffee, breakfast and tacos. Opened Oct. 12.
- **Lupita's Kitchen Bar & Grill**, 887 SH 21 W., Cedar Creek. The family-run Mexican restaurant's second location is in the former Leo's Italian Grill. Opened Sept. 20.
- **Magnolia Row Boutique**, 1003 Main St., downtown Bastrop. Apparel, jewelry and gifts in the historic Luck Drug Store building. Opened Sept. 5.
- **Si Baby-Q**, 865 FM 1209, Bastrop. Tex-Mex-style barbecue. Opened Aug. 7.
- **Doug's Boys Pig & Loaf**, 1148 SH 21, Bastrop. Sandwich-first barbecue, plus catering. Opened Aug. 29.

**Coming soon**

- **Fore Fun Saloon**, 834 SH 71 W. A private club with free membership, plus mini golf, live music and beer, in the former Back 9 Bar. Opening is planned for late 2026.
- **Taco Casa**, 1502 Chestnut St. The Texas fast-food chain hasn't set an opening date.
- **Burlington**, Burleson Crossing East, 655 SH 71 W. A $3.3 million, 22,520-square-foot building, with construction expected to run through Sept. 1, 2027.
- **Dick's Sporting Goods**, Burleson Crossing East at SH 71 and Edward Burleson Lane. An $11 million, 55,000-square-foot project, with work expected to run through Dec. 31, 2027.

**Closed**

- **Sugar Shack**, 114 Loop 150 W. The candy and fudge shop closed Nov. 2.

**For drivers:** Construction at Burleson Crossing East will add to activity along SH 71 over the next year. Project timelines come from state filings and can change.
