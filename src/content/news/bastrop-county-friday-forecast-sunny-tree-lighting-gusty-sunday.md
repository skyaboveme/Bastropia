The freeze has passed, and Bastrop County has a sunny Friday for holiday events, followed by a warmer Saturday and a windy, dry front on Sunday.

**Today and tonight**

The National Weather Service in Austin/San Antonio calls for a **sunny** Friday with a high near **68**. A light south-southwest wind increases to 5 to 10 mph in the afternoon and could gust as high as **20 mph**.

Tonight is **mostly clear**, with a low around **43** and a light south breeze. That's milder than Thanksgiving night, but you'll still want a jacket for evening events.

**Saturday**

Saturday looks **sunny** and warmer, with a high near **74**. South-southwest wind of 5 to 10 mph increases to 10 to 15 mph in the afternoon, and gusts could reach **30 mph**. Saturday night stays mild, around 54, before the wind shifts to the northwest after midnight, with gusts up to 25 mph.

**A gusty, dry front Sunday**

A cold front arrives Sunday **without rain**. The forecast calls for a mostly sunny day, a high near **64**, and a north wind of 10 to 20 mph, **gusting as high as 35 mph**.

In its early-morning discussion, the weather service said Sunday's winds may come **within a few mph of wind-advisory criteria**. No wind advisory was in effect for Bastrop County as of Friday morning. Sunday night falls to about 38.

**Rain may return late next week**

After a sunny, milder Monday and Tuesday — with Tuesday's high near **76** — the point forecast for Bastrop gives a **40 percent** chance of showers Wednesday night and a **60 percent** chance of showers Thursday, when the high is near 77. The weather service's area discussion said those rain chances look most likely in the eastern half of its forecast area, which includes Bastrop County.

**What's happening while you're out**

County and city offices in Bastrop, Elgin and Smithville are **closed today** for the Thanksgiving holiday. Closures and trash changes: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-2026-closures-trash-schedule

- **Rivals for a Cause** is 1 to 10 p.m. on Main Street in downtown Bastrop, with the Texas A&M-Texas game on a big screen at 6:30 p.m. Details: https://bastropia.com/news/black-friday-2026-bastrop-rivals-for-a-cause-optoutside-hike
- **Bastrop's tree lighting** is 6 to 8 p.m. at Fisherman's Park, and the River of Lights opens the same night, 6 to 10 p.m. through Jan. 4. Details: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-tree-lighting-river-of-lights-friday-nov-27-2026

**What to do**

- Tie down holiday decorations and inflatables before Saturday's and Sunday's gusts.
- With dry, windy weather Sunday, take extra care with anything that could spark an outdoor fire. No burn ban was listed for the county as of Thursday, Nov. 26; check the county page before you burn.
- If you're going to the tree lighting, dress for a night in the 40s.
