Classic cars, hot rods and a ceremony honoring veterans filled downtown Bastrop on Saturday, Nov. 14, for the annual Heroes & Hotrods Car Show, according to photo coverage in the [Austin American-Statesman](https://www.statesman.com/news/local/bastrop/article/bastrop-heroes-hotrods-vintage-car-show-photos-22534037.php).

**Honoring local veterans**

The Statesman reported that Marine Corps veteran Roy Morris was named **NCO Veteran of the Year**, recognized for his "outstanding contribution and service to our Country, and to Bastrop and its citizens." Retired Marine Col. **Frank Goral** was named **Officer Veteran of the Year**.

The veterans ceremony took place on the courthouse lawn. Members of Bastrop ISD's Navy Junior ROTC took part, and the Honor Choir of Bastrop performed.

**On Main Street**

The show is sponsored by the Bastrop Area Cruisers. It drew classic cars, hot rods, custom builds, trucks and muscle cars, plus food vendors, shopping, awards and a Best of Show competition. Some entries came from well outside the county. The Statesman noted that a 1957 Chevrolet two-door hardtop owned by Richard Mayer of Plano won best 1950s-era sedan.
