Thanksgiving is starting cold in Bastrop County, but a warm-up is on the way for the long weekend.

**Freeze warning until 9 a.m.**

The National Weather Service in Austin/San Antonio put Bastrop County under a **freeze warning from 3 a.m. to 9 a.m. Thursday**. Forecasters expected temperatures as low as **31 degrees**, cold enough to damage tender plants and unprotected outdoor pipes.

Forecasters said this would be the first freeze of the season for much of the I-35 corridor and the counties just east of it. They noted that most urban areas usually stay just above freezing in a first freeze, while open and low-lying areas get colder.

**Thanksgiving Day**

Expect a **sunny** holiday with a high near **58**. A north-northwest breeze of 5 to 10 mph could gust to 25 mph, so it will feel cooler in the shade.

Thursday night turns partly cloudy and cold again, with a low around **33**. If you covered plants or dripped faucets last night, plan to do it again tonight.

**Friday and Saturday warm up**

- **Friday:** Mostly sunny, high near **66**. Friday night is mostly clear with a low around **40**, so bring a jacket to evening events like Bastrop's tree lighting.
- **Saturday:** Sunny and warmer, high near **73**. South-southwest wind of 5 to 20 mph could gust to **35 mph**. Saturday night stays mild, around 53.

**A dry, gusty front Sunday**

A cold front arrives Sunday without rain. The forecast calls for a mostly sunny day with a high near **66** and northwest wind of 15 to 20 mph, gusting to **35 mph**. Sunday night falls to about 37.

The weather service said it does not expect any new freezes after this front. Its forecasters said the next chance of rain may not come until late next week.

**What to do**

- Protect plants, pets and outdoor pipes again Thursday night.
- Tie down holiday decorations and inflatables before Saturday's and Sunday's gusts.
- With dry, windy days ahead, take extra care with anything that could spark outdoor fires.
