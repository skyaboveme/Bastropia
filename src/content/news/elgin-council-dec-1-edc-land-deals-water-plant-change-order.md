Elgin City Council has posted its agenda for **Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 6:30 p.m.** The meeting is at the Elgin Public Library Annex council chambers, 404 N. Main St., and can be watched on the city's YouTube channel.

Several items involve land the Elgin Economic Development Corporation (EDC) owns. Here is what's on the list, based on the agenda and meeting packet.

**City would buy two properties from the EDC**

The council will consider buying two tracts from the EDC: **412 Depot Street** and a tract on **North Avenue A**, with the buildings on them. The price is **$3,110,000** in cash, paid at a closing set for Oct. 31, 2027. The EDC board is scheduled to review the deal earlier the same day.

**A business park sale tied to 30 jobs**

The EDC board has approved selling Lot 12, about **5.88 acres** in Elgin Business Park III, to Ember STIC LLC for $1,024,531.20. The council is asked to approve it too.

According to the packet, the company plans a pipe spool fabrication shop, a warehouse and an engineering and design office, with about **30** new full-time jobs. The deal requires at least 30,000 square feet of industrial space and at least $2.5 million in investment.

**More work at the water plant**

In August, the council hired a contractor to expand the city's water treatment plant. Change order No. 1 would add **$817,000** in work picked from the contractor's alternate bid list. The change order form puts the new contract total at $6,292,107 and adds 96 days to the schedule.

The packet lists $1,970,700 in Texas General Land Office grant money and $5,104,990 from the city for the project.

**Other items**

- A **$14,000** fee waiver request from St. Peter's Lutheran Church for its nativity in Veterans Memorial Park. The live nativity is Dec. 5, and the scene stays up through Dec. 27. Staff made no recommendation.
- A new reserve policy that would keep about 90 days, or roughly 25%, of General Fund operating spending in reserve.
- An agreement to have a vendor strip and sell retired police vehicles.
- A new Visitor's Center agreement with the Elgin Chamber of Commerce, paid from hotel tax funds.
- A staff update on the Mary Christian Burleson Homestead, which the city received Oct. 31.

**The EDC meets first**

The EDC board meets at **5 p.m.** Dec. 1 at Railroad Ranch, 412 Depot Ave. It has a public hearing on selling about 2.02 acres at 201 Roy Rivers Road to ZT Global Investments for medical buildings totaling about 8,000 square feet.

**How to speak**

To speak at the council meeting, fill out a public comment form and give it to the City Secretary before the meeting starts. Comments are in person and limited to three minutes. Emailed comments go to council members but are not read aloud.
