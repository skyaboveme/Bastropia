Burlington Cos. filed Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation papers on Oct. 21 for a store in Bastrop’s Burleson Crossing East. The Bastrop EDC’s summary puts the job at about $3.3 million for a 22,520-square-foot retail building, with construction listed from Nov. 9, 2026, through Sept. 1, 2027.

Dick’s Sporting Goods is also slated for that center. Filing dates are not opening dates.
