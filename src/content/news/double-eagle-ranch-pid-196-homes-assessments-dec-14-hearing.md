Families who buy the last 196 homes planned at Double Eagle Ranch would pay a yearly assessment on top of their property taxes. Bastrop County has scheduled a public hearing on those charges for **Monday, Dec. 14**.

**What a PID is**

A public improvement district, or PID, lets the county borrow money for a subdivision's public improvements, such as roads. The debt is then repaid through assessments on the lots that benefit.

The Double Eagle Ranch district is in unincorporated Bastrop County, in the City of Bastrop's extraterritorial jurisdiction. Its third and final phase is Riverbend at Double Eagle, a Meritage Homes subdivision, The Texas Dispatch reported. The improvements include a bridge.

**What buyers would pay**

According to the preliminary plan the Dispatch reviewed:

- Each of the **196** lots would carry a maximum assessment of **$33,091.84**, or $6,486,000 in all.
- The offering document estimates a finished home's value at $380,500.
- At that value, the Dispatch estimates the yearly assessment at about **$2,984**.

The document says the figures are preliminary and could change.

**Who backs the bonds**

The bonds would be "payable solely from" the assessments, the offering document says, so the county's general taxpayers would not back them. The developer recovers its spending on the improvements through the bond sale.

The district's first two phases have 105 and 181 lots and were financed by earlier bonds, the Dispatch reported.

**What's on the calendar**

The county's Nov. 23 agenda included a resolution setting the costs, approving a preliminary assessment plan and calling the Dec. 14 hearing. A second item covered a preliminary offering document for 2027 bonds. Results of that meeting were not yet posted early Tuesday.

At the hearing, the court must hear objections and may change any parcel's assessment before it votes. Notice goes to current property owners. The Dispatch noted that families who buy later will not get that notice.

**If you're shopping for a home**

Texas law requires a seller to give written notice of PID assessments before a purchase contract is signed, the document says. Ask for that notice and the yearly amount before you sign.

**How to weigh in**

Commissioners Court meets at 804 Pecan St. in Bastrop. To speak, fill out the county's Public Participation Form and give it to the County Clerk before the item comes up.
