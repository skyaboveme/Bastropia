**The deal**

Singulus Technologies, a German company that builds industrial manufacturing equipment, announced Nov. 10 that it signed a supply contract with SpaceX. Singulus will provide equipment for a production line for heterojunction solar cells, a type of high-efficiency cell. The first line is expected to be delivered in the first half of 2027, according to the Courier. Singulus said the partnership includes plans for more lines.

A SpaceX executive said in a statement that the partnership gives the company a manufacturing path for solar cells for its satellites at its production facility in Texas. SpaceX runs a satellite manufacturing facility at **858 FM 1209** in Bastrop County.

**What the county knows**

Not much yet. County officials told the Courier they weren't told about the agreement before it was announced. "At this time, Bastrop County does not have sufficient information regarding the agreement," said Lyndsey Schroeder, executive assistant to County Judge Gregory Klaus.

The companies haven't said how many jobs the deal could bring.

**Roads to watch**

According to Schroeder:

- SpaceX has been working with TxDOT on infrastructure needs along **FM 1209** and at its intersection with **FM 969**.
- SpaceX and the county have had preliminary talks about extending and expanding **Walker-Watson Road** and **Blue Flame Road** to ease local congestion and improve safety.

Schroeder said those talks are preliminary, and no decisions or commitments have been made.

**Why it matters**

The FM 1209 corridor has grown quickly. If you live or commute near FM 1209 and FM 969, these are the roads to keep an eye on. Any county road project would come before Commissioners Court, and Bastropia will share it when it's on an agenda.
