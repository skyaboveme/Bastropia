A Bastrop-based nonprofit, Troops Need Roofs, helps veterans, active-duty service members and military families get roofs replaced or repaired at no cost, and it's taking applications and nominations, [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/business/from-service-to-support-free-roof-repairs-for-bastrop-veterans/) reported.

**How it works**

Community Impact reported that the nonprofit was started in partnership with Mottco Roofing & Construction, a veteran-owned company. The company's leader, Steve Moffitt, chairs the nonprofit's board. Executive director Steve Coon, a former Texas Army National Guard officer who deployed to Iraq, coordinates the work. Besides roofs, the group helps with home safety improvements.

Applications are reviewed based on:

- the condition of the roof
- insurance status and inspection findings
- financial hardship

Having homeowners insurance does **not** automatically disqualify you. Families of service members who died while serving can also apply.

**Know a veteran who needs help?**

Veterans can apply for themselves, or neighbors, family and friends can nominate them. Coon told Community Impact that asking for help "is a sign of strength, not weakness."

**Other ways to help**

The nonprofit is a 501(c)(3), so donations are tax-deductible. It also welcomes volunteers, and contractors and businesses can donate labor or materials.

- **Website:** [troopsneedroofs.org](https://www.troopsneedroofs.org)
- **Mailing address:** 489 Agnes St., Suite 112-360, Bastrop
