**The proposal**

The Central Texas Regional Mobility Authority and the Texas Department of Transportation are studying an extension of the 290 Toll from its current end near SH 130 in Travis County to **SH 95 South in Bastrop County**. The plan would:

- Add **tolled main lanes** next to the existing free lanes from SH 130 to SH 95 South
- Improve intersections
- Add bicycle and pedestrian improvements

The project covers about **15 miles**. The Mobility Authority's 2025 report to the Texas Transportation Commission put the preliminary cost at about **$1.5 billion**, Community Impact reported.

**Where it stands**

The two agencies started studying the US 290 corridor in April 2025. They showed preliminary design plans at open houses on Sept. 29 and Oct. 1. The public comment period for that round closed Oct. 30.

**What's next**

- **Mid-2027:** preliminary findings from the environmental review are expected.
- **After that:** a public hearing, where residents can comment again.
- **Early 2028:** a decision on whether the project can move forward.

**Why it matters here**

US 290 is the main road between eastern Travis County and Elgin, and traffic on it has grown along with the area. Free lanes would stay under the proposal, so drivers who don't want to pay a toll would still have a way through.

The public hearing is the next real chance to weigh in. Bastropia will share the date when it's announced.
