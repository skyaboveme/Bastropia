A rancher's herd between Paige and Smithville lost five animals last week, and investigators are asking neighbors for help.

The Texas and Southwestern Cattle Raisers Association (TSCRA) says **four cows and one bull** were shot and killed on a property off **FM 2104 on Graysville Road**, between Paige and Smithville in Bastrop County. It happened sometime between **Nov. 16 and Nov. 17**, according to the association's Crime Watch report.

**A reward is offered**

TSCRA is offering a **$2,500** reward for information that leads to the arrest of whoever killed the cattle.

**How to share a tip**

If you saw or heard something in that area those days, such as gunshots, an unfamiliar vehicle or people near the fence line, call:

- TSCRA Special Ranger Kenny Murchison: **512-705-3226**
- Operation Cow Thief tip line: **817-916-1775**

TSCRA's special rangers investigate livestock and ranch crimes across Texas. Murchison covers District 22, which the association lists as part of East Texas. The Operation Cow Thief line takes tips on livestock theft and other ranch crimes.

**For landowners nearby**

The report did not say whether anyone has been identified. If you keep livestock in the area, it's a good time to:

- Walk your fences and check on animals more often.
- Note license plates of vehicles that stop or idle near pastures, and keep the information handy for investigators.
- Report anything suspicious rather than confronting anyone yourself.

**Why it matters**

Cattle are a big part of rural Bastrop County's economy, and losing a bull and four cows is a real cost for a family operation. Shooting livestock that belongs to someone else is a crime in Texas.

Bastropia will follow up if TSCRA or local authorities release more information.
