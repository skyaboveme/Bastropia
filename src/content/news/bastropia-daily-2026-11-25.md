Good morning, Bastrop County. The National Weather Service expects a sunny Wednesday with a high near 58 and north-northwest wind gusts up to 20 mph. Tonight turns clear and cold, with a low around 32, so bring pets and tender plants in. Thanksgiving Day looks sunny with a high near 59, and Friday warms to about 65. There were no weather alerts early this morning.

**Today's stories**

- **What's closed for Thanksgiving:** County and city offices close Thursday and Friday; Smithville's close at noon today. Bastrop trash pickup slides one day, and the Bastrop library closes at 5 p.m. today. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-2026-closures-trash-schedule
- **New signal at FM 969 and FM 1209:** TxDOT expects it working by Nov. 30, part of $1.12 million in SpaceX-funded upgrades. https://bastropia.com/news/fm-969-fm-1209-traffic-signal-by-nov-30-spacex-funded-upgrades
- **Cattle shot near Paige:** Four cows and a bull were killed Nov. 16–17 off FM 2104 on Graysville Road; there's a $2,500 reward. https://bastropia.com/news/cattle-shot-killed-paige-smithville-graysville-road-2500-reward
- **Small Business Saturday:** Wassail Fest in downtown Bastrop, 3 to 7 p.m., and Shop Small in downtown Elgin, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. https://bastropia.com/news/small-business-saturday-2026-wassail-fest-bastrop-shop-small-elgin

**Coming up**

- **Today, Wednesday, Nov. 25:** Calvary Episcopal serves its 39th Thanksgiving Eve dinner from 4 to 6 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary-2026-11-25
- **Friday, Nov. 27:** Bastrop's tree lighting at Fisherman's Park, 6 to 8 p.m., and the River of Lights opens for the season. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-s-annual-tree-lighting-2026-2026-11-27

**Church and community**

The Pentecostals of Bastrop, 261 FM 20, will livestream Sunday worship on Nov. 29, the first Sunday of Advent; recent messages, including "My God Shall Supply," are on the church's YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCsh-GCAlLiVJs7E8Ags1UDg. In Elgin, St. Peter's Lutheran Church plans a live nativity in Veterans Memorial Park on Saturday, Dec. 5.

**At the county and city halls**

County offices are closed Thursday and Friday. No new Commissioners Court agenda had posted early Wednesday; public hearings are already set for Monday, Dec. 14, including the Double Eagle Ranch assessments and a Mesa Vista replat, and for Dec. 28 on a Hidden Oaks replat. The court meets at 804 Pecan St., Bastrop. In Elgin, the EDC board meets Dec. 1 at 5 p.m. and City Council at 6:30 p.m. Agendas: https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/.
