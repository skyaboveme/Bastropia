It's a Class 5A Division II game in the UIL playoffs. Bastrop reached the area round by beating Victoria West 62–43 in the bi-district round. KBTX reported that Huntsville got past Nederland 42–39.

**If you're going**

- **When:** Friday, Nov. 20, 7 p.m.
- **Where:** Rhodes Stadium, Katy
- **Tickets:** The schedules we found don't include ticket details. Check Bastrop ISD and Bastrop High School athletics channels for presale information, prices and any rules on clear bags or digital tickets.

Katy is a long drive east of Bastrop, and Friday-evening traffic around Houston can be heavy. Give yourself extra time and confirm the site before you leave. Playoff sites sometimes change.

**Can't make the trip?** Watch Bastrop ISD and the team's social channels for stream or radio information. We'll share it here if it's announced.

The winner moves on to the regional quarterfinals. Go Bears!
