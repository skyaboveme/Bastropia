Bastrop CATS will hold its **11th annual Kitty BINGO** fundraiser on **Saturday, Nov. 21**, at the Bastrop Convention & Exhibit Center, 1408 Chestnut St. This year's theme is "Rhinestone Rodeo." The details below come from Community Impact's Nov. 18 report and the event's Bastrop County Calendar listing.

**What to know**

- **When:** Saturday, Nov. 21. VIP entry starts at 4:30 p.m.; general admission runs from 5:15 to 9 p.m.
- **Where:** Bastrop Convention & Exhibit Center, 1408 Chestnut St., Bastrop
- **Cost:** $65 for general admission, $95 for VIP
- **What's included:** A buffet dinner, door prizes, raffles, a live auction and 12 rounds of bingo
- **Dress code (optional):** Sparkly Western wear. There's a themed costume contest.
- **Tickets and updates:** The Bastrop CATS Facebook page and the event listing at https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/kitty-bingo-rhinestone-rodeo-2026

**Where the money goes**

Bastrop CATS is a group of local volunteers who trap stray and free-roaming cats, have them spayed or neutered and vaccinated, and return them, an approach known as trap-neuter-return. Proceeds from Kitty BINGO pay for that work and for emergency medical care, the group told Community Impact.

Board secretary Launa Johnson told Community Impact that the group has helped spay or neuter and vaccinate **more than 12,000 cats** since it was founded in 2014, including **more than 1,700** so far in 2026.

**Remembering a co-founder**

This year's event comes days after the death of Bastrop CATS co-founder and director **Terrie Lynn Bach** on Nov. 13. Johnson told Community Impact that Bach spent more than a decade caring for homeless cats in Bastrop County.

**Can't make it?**

If you can't attend but want to help community cats, contact Bastrop CATS through its Facebook page to ask about donating or volunteering.
