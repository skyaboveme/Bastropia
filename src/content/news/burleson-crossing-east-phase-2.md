Burleson Crossing East’s second and final phase of construction was listed to start Monday, Nov. 9, at 655 W. State Highway 71. MySA’s read of Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation filings shows three Phase II jobs totaling about $10.8 million.

One filing covers a nearly 42,000-square-foot retail building at about $6 million, with a finish date of Aug. 15, 2027. Another is the $3.3 million, 22,520-square-foot Burlington already noted on this desk. A third is a roughly 10,000-square-foot shop shell at about $1.5 million, also aimed at mid-August 2027.

The wider Burleson Crossing roster already includes names like Hobby Lobby, Petco, TJ Maxx, Ulta Beauty, Spec’s, Ross, and Staples. State papers set a construction window, not a store opening day.
