Texas' environmental agency is reviewing data center permits again, ending a pause that had been in place since September. That matters in Cedar Creek, where neighbors are waiting on a state decision about a proposed gas-fired power plant that would serve a data center campus.

**What changed**

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) said it "has resumed its normal permit review process," The Texas Tribune reported Nov. 17. FOX 7 Austin reported the same statement Nov. 18.

According to the Tribune, Gov. Greg Abbott asked the state grid operator, ERCOT, in August to audit data centers trying to connect to the grid. In a Sept. 21 letter, he asked TCEQ to hold permit approvals until that review was done. TCEQ said it was told on Friday, Nov. 13, that ERCOT had finished gathering information on community impacts.

TCEQ handles air pollution permits and construction stormwater permits for these projects.

**What's still ahead**

ERCOT's findings are not public yet. The Tribune reported that ERCOT plans to give its full findings to the Public Utility Commission on Dec. 10 and discuss them at the commission's Dec. 17 open meeting. ERCOT said in early November that 93% of 465 projects had responded to its information request.

Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa, who defeated Abbott on Nov. 3, said during the campaign she would keep data center approvals paused until lawmakers pass new rules. She takes office in January. Environmental groups, including Public Citizen, have criticized TCEQ for resuming permits before the findings are released.

**The Cedar Creek connection**

In August, Pacifico CCK Energy 1 LLC applied to TCEQ for a standard air permit for a natural-gas power plant at 382 Earl Callahan Road. The application lists 48 turbines. Bastrop County asked TCEQ in October to hold a public meeting, and KEYE reported Nov. 19 that 73 neighbors signed a letter asking for a public hearing.

The news reports do not say whether the pause held up this application or when TCEQ might decide. Residents can check its status with TCEQ under Regulated Entity Number RN102987831.

Bastropia will follow the Dec. 10 findings and any TCEQ action on the Cedar Creek application.
