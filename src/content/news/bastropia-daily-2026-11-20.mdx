**Today's stories**

- **Cedar Creek neighbors want a hearing:** Seventy-three residents near Earl Callahan Road signed a letter seeking a public hearing on a proposed natural-gas power plant and data center campus. The state air permit, which lists 48 turbines, is still under review. https://bastropia.com/news/cedar-creek-data-center-gas-plant-neighbors-letter
- **Old Iron Bridge reopens Dec. 3:** After about $11 million in repairs, Bastrop's 1923 truss bridge reopens to walkers with a love-locks sculpture at 4 p.m., a ribbon cutting at 4:30 and new lights at dusk. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-old-iron-bridge-reopens-dec-3
- **Operation Turkey needs volunteers:** The Elgin team is aiming for 3,200 Thanksgiving meals and adding Bastrop and Coupland deliveries. Drivers are especially needed on Nov. 26. https://bastropia.com/news/operation-turkey-elgin-3200-thanksgiving-meals-2026
- **Christmas tree farms:** Elgin Christmas Tree Farm opens Saturday; two more nearby farms open Nov. 27. https://bastropia.com/news/christmas-tree-farms-near-bastrop-elgin-2026

**Tonight and this weekend**

- **Tonight:** Chili Cook-Off — Late Night at The Bodega, 6–11 p.m. at Hyperloop Plaza, 865 FM 1209, Bastrop, with live music from Trent Morgan, mechanical bull riding and 10+ chili entries. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/chili-cook-off-late-night-at-the-bodega-2026-11-20
- **Tonight:** The Bastrop Bears play Huntsville in the Class 5A Division II area round in Katy. Kickoff listings vary between 7 and 7:30 p.m., so check before you leave.
- **Saturday:** Rising Sun Vineyard's 7th annual Autumn Market Festival in McDade starts at 10 a.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/autumn-market-festival-2026-2026-11-21 Also Saturday: the Trot with Tracy's 5K for CASA and Kitty BINGO for Bastrop CATS. More weekend picks: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-weekend-nov-20-22-2026
