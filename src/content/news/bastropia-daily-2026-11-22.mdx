Good morning, Bastrop County. The National Weather Service expects a slight chance of showers early and patchy fog, then a mostly sunny Sunday with a high near 73. West-northwest winds could gust to 25 mph. It turns cooler tonight, with a low around 46 and gusty north winds, and Monday should be sunny with a high near 63. There were no weather alerts for Bastrop early this morning.

**Today's stories**

- **EdgeConneX tax talk Monday:** County commissioners will discuss possible tax abatements for the Cedar Creek data center developer. At the Capitol, lawmakers want to end a state data center sales tax break. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-edgeconnex-tax-break-talk-state-data-center-exemption
- **Texas 71 addresses could change:** A county committee wants one numbering system for the highway so 911 calls aren't sent to the wrong place. Nearly 1,700 addresses could be affected, starting in August 2027. https://bastropia.com/news/texas-71-readdressing-bastrop-county-911-1700-addresses
- **Winter Blast sign-ups:** Bastrop ISD's free coat, toy, book and food giveaway is Dec. 17 at Cedar Creek High School. Register each child for toys by Dec. 1. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-isd-winter-blast-2026-toy-registration-dec-1
- **Thanksgiving week planner:** Calvary's Thanksgiving Eve dinner, Bastrop's tree lighting and River of Lights, Wassail Fest and more. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-week-2026-tree-lighting-wassail-fest
