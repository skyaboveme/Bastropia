Bastrop ISD has started a program that lets some of its own employees earn a bachelor's degree and a Texas teaching certificate without paying tuition, [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/education/bastrop-isd-launches-tuition-free-program-to-certify-educators) reported.

**What it is**

The district's Grow-Your-Own program began Oct. 26 with a first group of 10 educators. It's built on an accelerated program created by Indiana Wesleyan University and Teachworthy, a teacher certification organization, for school employees who don't yet have a degree. As a partner district, Bastrop ISD covers the tuition. Windy Burnett, the district's human resources special programs coordinator, told Community Impact the program takes one to three years to finish.

**Who can apply**

Any full-time Bastrop ISD employee who isn't certified can apply, according to Burnett. The district looked at each applicant's commitment to the district, ability to finish the program, the subject or grade they want to teach, readiness for the classroom, and communication and professionalism.

**How it works**

Participants can put several kinds of credit toward the degree: Teachworthy courses, college credits they already have, Indiana Wesleyan coursework and district professional development. After they finish their credits, they complete an internship through Teachworthy to earn certification.

**Why the district is doing this**

New state rules require teachers to hold proper certification. Community Impact reported that the Texas Education Agency released new certification requirements in August 2025 and gave Bastrop ISD until the 2029–30 school year to meet them. The district's certification support plan includes Grow-Your-Own.

The numbers show why it matters. In December 2025, Community Impact reported that 115 of the district's kindergarten through fifth-grade teachers weren't fully certified. Burnett said 97 pre-K through fifth-grade teachers are now working toward certification.

Burnett said the district plans to keep the program going and hopes to expand it in future rounds. Bastrop ISD employees who are interested should watch for the next application window and contact the district's human resources office.
