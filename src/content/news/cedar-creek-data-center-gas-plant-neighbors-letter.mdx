Neighbors of a proposed natural-gas power plant and data center campus in Cedar Creek are asking for a public hearing before the project wins state approval.

KEYE (CBS Austin) reported Thursday, Nov. 19, that **73 residents** who live within about a mile of Earl Callahan Road signed a letter to Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company that residents say would lease the data center. The letter asks the company to back a public hearing and to listen to neighbors' concerns. An Anthropic spokeswoman told the station the company is in talks but has not signed a lease.

**What is proposed**

The project is being developed by Pacifico CCK Energy, according to KEYE, which also reported that a company called Blackchamber is involved in development. A permit application filed with the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) in August describes a simple-cycle, natural-gas-fired power plant at 382 Earl Callahan Road. It lists **48 combustion turbines** from several manufacturers. KEYE reported in October that TCEQ filings put the plant's output at roughly 1,300 megawatts.

A company fact sheet cited by KEYE says the campus would include two data center buildings and the on-site power plant, with about 600 acres developed on a property of about 2,842 acres. No ground has been broken, and the project still needs permits.

**Why neighbors are worried**

Residents told KEYE they are concerned about noise, heat, air emissions, water use and the effect on wildlife and the rural landscape. Because the company applied under a standard permit, residents have pointed out that the process does not require public notice or a comment period. Some neighbors told the station they believe the companies want state permits approved before Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa takes office. KEYE reported that Hinojosa has signaled she would pause data center approvals, while projects approved earlier would be grandfathered.

**What the county has done**

On Oct. 13, Bastrop County commissioners approved a resolution asking TCEQ to hold a public meeting on the air permit. That request does not guarantee a meeting. Commissioners said at the time that their authority over the project is limited.

**How to follow it**

The application is filed under TCEQ Regulated Entity Number **RN102987831** (Pacifico CCK Energy 1 LLC), which residents can use to look up the permit's status with the agency.

A separate data center company, EdgeConnex, is on the agenda for discussion of possible tax breaks at Monday's Commissioners Court meeting, Nov. 23, at 9 a.m. at 804 Pecan St. in Bastrop. No vote is listed on that item. The agenda is at https://bastropco.civicweb.net/document/128202.
