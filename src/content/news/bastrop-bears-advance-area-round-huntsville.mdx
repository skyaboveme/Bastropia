According to [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/nDDByX2mfUCjMIO87ORpWQ/football-recap-victoria-west-falls-short-of-bastrop-in-the-playoffs--how-to-watch.htm), quarterback Weston Nielsen threw for 217 yards and six touchdowns and completed 76% of his passes. Dallas Crescenzo caught two of those touchdown passes. MaxPreps also noted that Victoria West gained 287 more yards than Bastrop but couldn't turn the extra yardage into points. The full game can be replayed on the NFHS Network, MaxPreps said.

The win extends a strong second half of the season. [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/bastrop/bastrop-bears/football/schedule/) lists the Bears at 7-4 overall, with a six-game winning streak and an unbeaten district record that put them first in their district. After a tough non-district stretch in September, Bastrop opened district play with a 70-0 win over Austin Crockett and beat county rival Elgin 51-0 on Oct. 30.

**What's next**

Bastrop advances to the area round of the Class 5A Division II, Region III playoffs against Huntsville. MaxPreps still listed the date and time as "TBA" Saturday. A playoff game-info thread on [SETXsports.com](https://www.setxsports.com/ip/topic/270002-area-round-game-infopost-here/) lists Friday, Nov. 20, at 7 p.m. at Katy Rhodes Stadium in Katy, but Bastropia hasn't confirmed that with the school yet. Another online listing gives a different date. Check Bastrop ISD and Bastrop High School athletics for the official date, time, venue and ticket details before you make travel plans. Playoff tickets are often sold online in advance.

We'll update this story when Bastrop ISD posts game and ticket information.
