Bastrop County Commissioners Court holds its regular terms on the second and fourth Mondays at the courthouse annex, 804 Pecan Street, unless the court has moved a holiday. Monday, October 27 is one of those days. The next date in the order is Monday, November 10.

The order sets the day and the room. It does not set the hour. That is on the agenda, which the county posts on its website.
