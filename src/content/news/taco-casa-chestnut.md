Taco Casa has posted a Bastrop location on Chestnut Street near Film Alley and Firestone Complete Auto Care, according to the Bastrop EDC’s write-up of the chain’s website. It would be the brand’s first restaurant in the county.

A website placeholder is not a permit or a soft-open date. Watch city and TDLR filings for construction papers.
