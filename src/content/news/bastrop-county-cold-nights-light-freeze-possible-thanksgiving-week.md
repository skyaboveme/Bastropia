Bastrop County is in for a run of cold nights this week, and a light freeze is possible on Thanksgiving Eve.

**The forecast**

The National Weather Service forecast for Bastrop, issued early Tuesday, calls for:

- **Tuesday:** Partly sunny, high near 54. North wind 10 to 15 mph with gusts up to 30 mph, and a 20% chance of showers after 3 p.m.
- **Tuesday night:** Partly cloudy, low near **33**, with gusts up to 25 mph.
- **Wednesday:** Sunny, high near 58.
- **Wednesday night:** Clear, low near **31**, with almost no wind.
- **Thanksgiving Day:** Sunny, high near **59**.
- **Thursday night:** Partly cloudy, low near 33.

No weather watches, warnings or advisories were in effect early Tuesday.

**A quick cold-night checklist**

A low near 31 is a light freeze, cold enough to hurt tender plants and be hard on animals. Before you go to bed Wednesday:

- **Pets:** Bring them inside or give them a dry, wind-free shelter with fresh water that won't freeze.
- **Plants:** Cover tender plants and bring potted ones in. Remove covers once it warms up Thursday morning.
- **Pipes:** Wrap exposed outdoor faucets and pipes, especially on pier-and-beam homes and well houses.
- **Livestock:** Check that water troughs aren't iced over in the morning.
- **Neighbors:** Check on older neighbors who live alone, and use space heaters only with plenty of clearance.

**Thanksgiving week plans**

The sunny holiday forecast is good news for outdoor plans, including Friday's tree lighting at Fisherman's Park and the opening of the River of Lights. Our Thanksgiving week guide has times and places: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-week-2026-tree-lighting-wassail-fest
