If you're looking for something to do Saturday morning before the shops get busy, Smithville's annual Turkey Trot is a short drive down SH 71.

**Smithville Turkey Trot**

- **Saturday, Nov. 28, 9 to 11:30 a.m.**
- **Smithville Recreation Center, 106 Gazley St.**
- Run, jog or walk a **5K or 1 mile**. Strollers and leashed dogs are welcome, according to Explore Bastrop County.

The event is organized by the Smithville Parks and Recreation Department, 512-237-3282, ext. 7. A 2026 online registration page was not posted on the city's events page as of Friday morning, so call the department to confirm how to sign up, the cost, and where the course starts.

Listing: https://explorebastropcounty.com/calendar-event/smithville-turkey-trot-2026/

**Saturday in the parks**

The Bastrop County Calendar also lists two midmorning outdoor programs:

- **10 a.m., Bastrop State Park:** "Thankful for the CCC," a ranger program about the Civilian Conservation Corps crews who built the park, including a chance to try building a block arch. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/thankful-for-the-ccc-2026-11-28
- **10 a.m., Buescher State Park in Smithville:** "Trail Mix: Edible Plants Guided Hike," led by a Lost Pines Master Naturalist. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/trail-mix-edible-plants-guided-hike-2026-11-28

Park entry fees apply at both parks. Check Texas Parks and Wildlife or the park entrance station for Saturday hours and any program changes.

**Later Saturday**

- **Rockne Market Day**, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. at 4045 FM 535. https://bastropia.com/news/thanksgiving-weekend-2026-bastrop-county-rockne-market-day-museum-holiday-exhibit
- **Shop Small Saturday** in downtown Elgin, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and **Lost Pines Wassail Fest** in downtown Bastrop. Discover Bastrop lists Wassail Fest from **3 to 7 p.m.** (registration opens at 2 p.m.). https://bastropia.com/news/small-business-saturday-2026-wassail-fest-bastrop-shop-small-elgin

**Weather**

Saturday looks sunny with a high near **74** and south-southwest gusts up to **30 mph**, according to the National Weather Service. Bring water, and have a plan to keep tents, signs and decorations in place if you're at an outdoor event.
