Today is Election Day in Bastrop County. The county schedule lists polls open from 7 am to 7 pm. A voter can use any Election Day location, including the courthouse annex at 804 Pecan Street in Bastrop, the Smithville Recreation Center, the Cedar Creek annex, and the Elgin Recreation Center.

Community Impact reports that CARTS will offer free rides to and from polling places in Bastrop County today. The code is VoteTexas2026 in the CARTS Country Bus or CARTS NOW app. Riders can also call [512-478-7433](tel:5124787433).

[See hours and polling places](/go-vote). The schedule is on [bastropvotes.org](https://www.bastropvotes.org/voting-schedule/). The ride offer is in [Community Impact’s report](https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/election/carts-to-offer-free-election-day-rides/).
