Early voting ended Friday, October 30. The next chance to vote in person is Election Day, Tuesday, November 3. The county schedule lists polls open from 7 am to 7 pm. On that day a voter can use any Election Day location in the county, including the courthouse annex at 804 Pecan Street in Bastrop, the Smithville Recreation Center, the Cedar Creek annex, and the Elgin Recreation Center.

Community Impact reports that CARTS will offer free rides to and from polling places in Bastrop County that day. The code is VoteTexas2026 in the CARTS Country Bus or CARTS NOW app. Riders can also call [512-478-7433](tel:5124787433).
