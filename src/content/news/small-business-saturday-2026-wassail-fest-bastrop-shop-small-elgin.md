The weekend after Thanksgiving is a big one for local shops. Here's where to spend Small Business Saturday, **Nov. 28**, in Bastrop County.

**Lost Pines Wassail Fest in downtown Bastrop**

Main Street merchants are hosting the annual Lost Pines Wassail Fest from **3 to 7 p.m.** Saturday, according to Community Impact. Admission is free.

- **Registration** opens at 2 p.m. at Main Street's intersections with Pine Street and Spring Street.
- The **first 1,000** guests to register get a souvenir cup and tote bag, according to Discover Bastrop.
- Sample wassail as you browse dozens of downtown shops, and vote for your favorite in the People's Choice contest.

Parking downtown fills up on festival days, so give yourself extra time.

**Shop Small Saturday in downtown Elgin**

Elgin's historic downtown is marking Shop Small Saturday from **8 a.m. to 8 p.m.**, according to the city's calendar, with 109 Depot St. listed as the location. Check individual shops for their own hours and deals.

**Also this weekend**

- **Friday, Nov. 27:** Bastrop lights its Christmas tree at Fisherman's Park, 1200 Willow St., from 6 to 8 p.m., with Bastrop ISD choirs and Santa arriving on a fire truck, according to Community Impact. The River of Lights opens that night along the riverwalk and runs 6 to 10 p.m. daily through **Jan. 4**.
- **Saturday, Nov. 28:** Rockne Market Day is listed at 4045 FM 535. See the listing for details: https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/rockne-market-day-2026-2026-11-28

**Why it matters**

Money spent at locally owned shops tends to stay in the community, supporting the owners and workers who live here. If you can't make it Saturday, many downtown stores are open all holiday season.
