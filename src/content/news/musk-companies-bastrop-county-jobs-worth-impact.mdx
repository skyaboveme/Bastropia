Since The Boring Company arrived off FM 1209 in 2021, a cluster of Elon Musk's companies now sits between Bastrop and the Colorado River. Here is what each one does in Bastrop County, how many people it employs here where a number has been published, what the company is worth, and what residents have gained and questioned.

**First, the names**

The name "SpaceXAI" is real. SpaceX acquired Musk's AI company xAI on Feb. 2, 2026, in a deal that valued SpaceX at $1 trillion and xAI at $250 billion, Reuters reported. xAI already owned X, the former Twitter. The AI unit was renamed SpaceXAI in July and in October took the brand SpaceX Super Intelligence, or SpaceXSI. SpaceX went public on Nasdaq on June 12.

**SpaceX is the biggest local employer of the group**

SpaceX's tax manager told commissioners on May 26 that the plant employs 1,590 people, is hiring 100 to 150 more, and plans an $855 million expansion to move all Starlink kit production to Bastrop County, with new jobs averaging more than $75,000 a year, the Elgin Courier reported. The company said most new jobs are entry level and that it met a state rule that 35% of new hires come from economically disadvantaged areas. In March 2025, the governor's office awarded SpaceX a $17.3 million Texas Semiconductor Innovation Fund grant for a $280 million chip packaging expansion expected to add more than 400 jobs.

**Tax base and incentives**

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. was Bastrop County's largest taxpayer in 2025, with $338.2 million in taxable value, or 2.2% of the county total, according to the county's annual financial report. It was not on the list in 2016. County officials said SpaceX has not asked for or received a county property tax abatement. Commissioners did nominate the expansion for the state Enterprise Zone program, which could refund up to $3.75 million in state sales tax.

**Snailbrook and everyday life**

The Musk compound, often called Snailbrook, has a bodega, pub, salon and playground that some residents welcome, Bloomberg reported in December 2025. Boring plans more than 20 staff homes, a science center and a gym, and has pitched community soccer fields and a pedestrian tunnel linking downtown Bastrop to the river, quoted at about $7.5 million.

**Concerns on the record**

Growth has drawn pushback. In 2023, Boring and SpaceX sought permits to discharge treated wastewater into the Colorado River; after local opposition, they agreed to use the City of Bastrop's treatment plant instead. TCEQ fined Boring $11,876 in 2024 over erosion controls and stormwater, KVUE reported. At the May 26 meeting, some residents asked commissioners to slow industrial approvals until more environmental study is done, while others pointed to jobs and tax revenue.

**What we couldn't confirm**

No public source gives local headcounts for X, SpaceXAI, the new SpaceX plants or Ad Astra, or Bastrop CAD values for Boring's property. We found no credible Bastrop County project called Hypernova. Bastropia will update this story as new filings come in.
