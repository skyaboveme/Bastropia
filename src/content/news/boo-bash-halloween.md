Boo Bash is listed as a free Halloween event on Saturday, October 31, along North Main Street and Spring Street in downtown Bastrop. The public listing calls it an all-day gathering.

Street events change parking and pickup plans. If you are coming from Elgin, Smithville, or Cedar Creek, check the Bastropia group that morning for a last-minute note on closures.

The listing did not publish a minute-by-minute schedule here. Treat the streets as the destination, and confirm hours with the organizer if you need a specific time.
