This week's Eats Beat is a countywide look at happy hours and specials posted by restaurants and bars in Bastrop, Elgin and Smithville. The cards below come from each venue's own website or official specials page, checked Sunday, Nov. 15, and Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. A few places also name promotions without posting the day, time or price; those are noted as such.

## Worth confirming

A few other restaurants and bars are often mentioned for happy hours or weekly deals, but those details were not on the venue's own website during this check. We are not listing prices or times for them here. If you are headed to one of these places, ask at the counter or check the venue directly:

- In Bastrop, [Chuy's](https://www.chuys.com/happy-hour) at 909 W. State Highway 71, and [Neighbor's Kitchen & Yard](https://neighborstx.com/) at 601 Chestnut St., Bldg. C.
- In Elgin, [Millie's on Main](https://milliesonmaintx.com/) at 212 N. Main St., and Bob's Blue Collar Tavern at 18810 Highway 290 E.
- In Smithville, [The Front Room Wine Bar](https://frontroomwinebar.com/) at 116 Main St., Huebels at 207 NW 2nd St., and [The Birdsong Bar & Patio](https://www.thebirdsongbar.com/) at 1200 NE Loop 230.
- In Paige, [Roadhouse Paige](https://roadhousepaige.com/) at 147 Old Highway 20 E. The restaurant's Facebook page has advertised a weekday happy hour, but the official website does not currently list it.

This is the first Eats Beat column on Bastropia. We will keep checking official menus and specials pages and will update the list as venues post new deals. For more Bastrop County news, visit [Bastropia.com](https://bastropia.com) and follow the [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bastropia).
