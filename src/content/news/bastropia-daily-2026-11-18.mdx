**Today's stories**

- **What's on the county's Nov. 23 agenda:** The 2026 certified tax levy, a discussion of possible tax abatements for data center company EdgeConnex, new county roads in Los Milagros and a Cardinal Drive drainage contract. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-commissioners-court-nov-23-2026-agenda-preview
- **ESD 3's first month:** The county's new ambulance district handled 1,023 incidents in October, and about half of transported patients stayed local. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-esd-3-ambulance-first-month-1023-calls
- **290 Toll timeline:** The proposed 15-mile extension to SH 95 South faces environmental findings in mid-2027, a public hearing after that and a decision in early 2028. https://bastropia.com/news/290-toll-extension-bastrop-county-whats-next
- **SpaceX solar-cell deal:** A new supply contract points to more manufacturing on FM 1209, and early road talks are underway. https://bastropia.com/news/spacex-bastrop-solar-cell-deal-singulus-road-talks

**Around the county**

- **Rosanky:** LCRA and Bluebonnet Electric Cooperative awarded Rosanky Christian Academy a $25,000 Community Grant to replace its playground equipment, with a safer surface, a border and solar lighting. The academy is adding $13,624 in matching funds, and the space is open to the community. https://www.lcra.org/news/news-releases/lcra-bluebonnet-electric-co-op-award-25000-grant-to-rosanky-christian-academy-to-upgrade-community-playground/
- **Smithville:** Café Duende, a new coffee and croffle shop from the original owners of Hope Floats Cafe, opened Oct. 21 at 109 NW Second St., Community Impact reports. https://communityimpact.com/bastrop-cedar-creek/dining/croffles-and-coffee-caf-duende-debuts-in-smithville/
- **Elgin:** The City Council met Tuesday night with the canvass of the Nov. 3 alcohol election on its agenda. We'll share the official result once the city posts it.
