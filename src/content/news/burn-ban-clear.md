The Bastrop County outdoor burning page currently says there is no burn ban in effect. Recent orders on that page include September 28, September 14, and August 24, 2026.

A clear page is not a promise that the woods are safe. This county has lost neighborhoods to wildfire. If you burn, follow the state’s outdoor-burning rule, keep the fire small, and put it dead out. If a ban is posted later today, the county page wins over this story.

[Read the county burn-ban page](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/page/em.outdoor_burning) before you burn. Questions inside ESD No. 2 can go to [(844) 347-3373](tel:8443473373).
