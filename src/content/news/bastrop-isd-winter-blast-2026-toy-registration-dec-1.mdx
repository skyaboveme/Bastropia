Bastrop ISD's free holiday giveaway is coming back next month, and families who want toys need to sign up soon.

<StatBox value="Dec. 1" label="Deadline to register children for toys at Bastrop ISD's Winter Blast" facts="Event Thu., Dec. 17, 5–7 p.m. · Cedar Creek High School · Coats, toys, books, food, resources" source="Bastrop ISD Winter Blast flyer" sourceUrl="https://www.bisdtx.org/departments/community-relations/winter-blast" />

**The event**

- **When:** Thursday, Dec. 17, 5 to 7 p.m.
- **Where:** Cedar Creek High School, 793 Union Chapel Road, Cedar Creek
- **What:** Bastrop ISD and partner organizations will hand out **coats, toys, books and food**, plus information about community resources. The district calls it a free community event.

**Toys require registration**

To request a toy, fill out the district's online form, **one form per child**, by **Tuesday, Dec. 1**, according to the event flyer. The form is in English and Spanish: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfRX_P4LppwjcWEInLe437cJTvyxoOXtk2qa6cM0NUI3h-XLw/viewform

The district notes that registering does not guarantee a toy will be available, so it's best to sign up early.

**Questions**

Call the Bastrop ISD Family Resource Center at 512-772-7130, or contact Nataly Sanchez at 512-772-7151 or nsanchez@bisdtx.org.

**Want to help?**

Bastrop ISD is looking for sponsors and volunteers. You can donate toys, volunteer at the event or give money. The district asks every donor to fill out its sponsorship form on the Winter Blast page. Sponsors who give $500 or more will be recognized on the district's community relations social media and the event page. Sponsorship questions go to 512-772-7180.

Details and the flyer in English and Spanish: https://www.bisdtx.org/departments/community-relations/winter-blast

Looking for more ways to give this season? Operation Turkey Elgin is still serving Thanksgiving meals. Read more: https://bastropia.com/news/operation-turkey-elgin-3200-thanksgiving-meals-2026
