Good morning, Bastrop County. The National Weather Service expects a mostly sunny Monday with a high near 61 and north wind gusts up to 25 mph. Tuesday turns cooler, near 54, with a slight chance of showers and gusts up to 30 mph. Nights get cold this week: lows near 34 Tuesday night and 33 Wednesday night, so bring pets and tender plants inside. Thanksgiving looks sunny, with a high near 59. There were no weather alerts early this morning.

**Today's stories**

- **Wildfire brush grant:** Commissioners are set to accept $35,000 to plan voluntary brush thinning on 330 parcels next to Bastrop and Buescher state parks. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-fema-grant-wildfire-brush-thinning-near-state-parks
- **The county's 2026 tax roll:** About $56.4 million for the county and $11.8 million for the road district go to commissioners for approval. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-2026-property-tax-levy-56-million
- **Data center permits resume:** The state is reviewing data center permits again. A Cedar Creek gas plant application is still pending. https://bastropia.com/news/tceq-resumes-data-center-permits-what-it-means-for-cedar-creek
- **Black Friday in Bastrop:** Aggies vs. Longhorns on Main Street, a ranger-led park hike and two tree lightings. https://bastropia.com/news/black-friday-2026-bastrop-rivals-for-a-cause-optoutside-hike
