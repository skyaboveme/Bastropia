The City of Bastrop is getting ready to shore up part of the Colorado River's bank in town, and residents have until **Tuesday, Nov. 24**, to send written comments on the plan.

In a public notice posted Nov. 6, the city said an environmental review found the **Colorado River Streambank Stabilization** project would have **no significant impact** on the environment, so a full environmental impact statement is not required. The notice also says the city plans to ask the Texas General Land Office (GLO) on or about Nov. 25 to release federal disaster-mitigation grant money for the work.

**What would be built**

According to the notice, the project covers roughly **1,200 feet** of riverbank within the city. Work would include:

- About 450 feet of sheet-pile retaining wall
- Changes to an existing storm drain outfall
- A new guardrail
- Riprap (large rock), geotextile fabric, grading and native plants
- Smaller spot repairs along the rest of the stretch

The city says the goal is to reduce erosion and help the bank, public infrastructure and nearby property hold up better in future floods. The notice estimates about 0.2 acres would be disturbed. Coordinates in the notice place the work along the river close to downtown Bastrop.

**What it costs**

The project is estimated at **$3,044,900**: $2,358,000 in HUD Community Development Block Grant Mitigation funds administered by the GLO, plus $686,900 in city money.

**How to comment**

- **Written comments** go to the City of Bastrop, 1311 Chestnut St., Bastrop, TX 78602, and must arrive by Nov. 24. Say which part of the notice you're addressing.
- **Review the file:** The full Environmental Review Record is available at City Hall, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.
- **Questions:** City Manager Sylvia Carrillo-Trevino, 512-332-8800.

After the city submits its request, the GLO will accept objections for 15 days, but only on specific procedural grounds listed in the notice. Free oral interpretation of the grant documents is available at City Hall.

Read the full notice (English and Spanish) on the city's public notices page: https://www.cityofbastrop.org/page/city.public_notices
