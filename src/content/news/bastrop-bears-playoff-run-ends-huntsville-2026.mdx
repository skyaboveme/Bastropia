The Bastrop Bears' football season ended Friday night in Katy.

Huntsville beat Bastrop **51-37** in the Class 5A Division II area round at Rhodes Stadium on Friday, Nov. 20, according to final scores posted by KBTX and SETXsports.

<StatBox

The Bears reached the area round after a high-scoring bi-district win over Victoria West, 62-43, on Nov. 13.

Huntsville moves on to the regional semifinals, where it will face Fort Bend Crawford. Crawford advanced Friday with a 45-0 win over Texas City.
