Bastrop County is set to take its first step today toward a wildfire brush-thinning project in two neighborhoods next to Bastrop and Buescher state parks.

**What's on the agenda**

Bastrop County Commissioners Court meets at 9 a.m. today, Nov. 23, at 804 Pecan St. in Bastrop. One item asks the court to accept a Hazard Mitigation Grant Program award from the Texas Division of Emergency Management (TDEM) and sign the related documents. County staff recommend approval.

The award pays for phase 1 of what the state calls the Bastrop County State Park Adjacent Lands Fuels Reduction project. Phase 1 totals **$35,000**: a $26,250 federal share and an $8,750 local share. The award letter is dated Nov. 3.

**Where the work would be**

According to the county's agenda memo, the project covers two neighborhoods with heavy buildup of brush and dead wood. They sit less than a mile apart. One is just outside Bastrop State Park, and the other is just outside Buescher State Park (spelled "Bucher" in the memo). Both are near an existing fuel-reduction project. The memo does not name the neighborhoods.

The project area covers **1,658 acres and 330 parcels**. Crews would remove encroaching brush, dead vegetation and some targeted trees, then trim the lower limbs of trees that remain.

**It's voluntary**

Only lots whose owners sign participation agreements, and that have dense underbrush, would be treated. That means the number of properties depends on how many owners take part.

Phase 1 is planning, not cutting. It covers environmental and biological reviews, collecting signed agreements and updating the project's benefit-cost analysis. The state's award requires the county to send an updated scope of work and budget based on the number of owners who sign. The grant period runs from Oct. 5, 2026, to Oct. 1, 2029.

**Other public safety items today**

The consent agenda, which is usually approved in one vote, also includes:

- A **$500,000** grant from the state's Rural Sheriff's Office Salary Assistance Program, with no local match.
- Adding a new public safety radio tower in northeastern Bastrop County to the county's hazard mitigation plan. The plan entry lists an estimated cost of $5 million and rates it high priority.
- Updated policies for the county's Community Emergency Response Team volunteers.

**How to weigh in**

To speak, fill out the county's Public Participation Form and give it to the County Clerk before the court reaches the item. Speakers usually get three minutes. The county's main number is 512-581-7100.
