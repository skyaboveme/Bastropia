A company planning to build small jet engines in Bastrop says its factory here may end up bigger than first announced.

Acutronic Group, an aerospace and defense manufacturer, has secured **$100 million in private investment**, Community Impact reported Friday, Nov. 20. The round was announced in a Nov. 17 company news release and led by private equity firms InterAlpen Partners and Hale Capital Partners.

**What Acutronic makes**

Acutronic builds microturbines, small jet engines used in drones and other defense systems. The company says it runs Texas' only jet engine factory, at its Austin headquarters, which opened in September 2024. According to the release cited by Community Impact, the new money will go toward producing more engines and components, developing new technology, and expanding manufacturing and supply chains. The company also plans to start making two more engine models in 2027.

**What it means for Bastrop**

CEO Florian Aigrain told Community Impact the company is in advanced design on a factory on a **13.8-acre site in the Bastrop Business and Industrial Park**. Groundbreaking is expected in **2027**. Because of projected growth, he said, the building will likely be larger than the **20,000 square feet** originally planned.

Acutronic has about 100 employees and contractors today, most based in Austin, and Aigrain said he expects to double its workforce in the Austin-Bastrop area over the next few years.

**The local deal**

The Bastrop City Council approved an economic development agreement with Acutronic in October 2023. Under that deal, as Community Impact has reported, the company committed to invest at least $4 million in the facility and create at least 50 full-time jobs.

In June, the Bastrop Economic Development Corp. moved ahead with an application for $3 million in federal money for infrastructure at the industrial park, with plans to redirect $1 million of Acutronic's incentives toward those upgrades. That application went through public comment in August. Aigrain said Nov. 20 that the EDC is making progress on its infrastructure work; Community Impact said the EDC had not responded to a request for a grant update.

Bastropia will follow the project as it moves toward construction.
