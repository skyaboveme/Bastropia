If you'd rather skip the stores on Black Friday, Bastrop has a rivalry watch party, a backcountry hike and two tree lightings on Friday, Nov. 27.

**Rivals for a Cause on Main Street**

Downtown Bastrop hosts Rivals for a Cause from 1 to 10 p.m. on Main Street, according to Discover Bastrop. College football games play on a big screen all day.

- **1 p.m.:** Cornhole tournament. Bring a partner.
- **6:30 p.m.:** Texas A&M vs. Texas, the main event.

Organizers are asking people to pledge a dollar amount for every Aggie or Longhorn touchdown to support a group the event page calls CAC. There is also a raffle with autographed items.

**#OptOutside at Bastrop State Park**

Texas Parks and Wildlife lists Gobble Till You Wobble, a ranger-led 3.5-mile hike through parts of Bastrop State Park the public doesn't often see, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. The ranger will talk about the park's history and how staff manage its natural resources.

Know before you go:

- Registration is required. Contact Madalyn Miller at 512-718-0211 or madalyn.miller@tpwd.texas.gov.
- The hike is for adults 17 and older. No pets.
- Bring at least four 16-ounce bottles of water per person. There's no drinking water on the trail and no restroom at the meeting spot.
- Wear closed-toe shoes that can handle about an inch of water at a crossing.

Earlier in the week, the park's free Turkey for Your Table craft is Tuesday, Nov. 24, from 10 to 11 a.m. at the Copperas Creek group picnic shelter. Park entry fees apply.

**Tree lightings and lights**

The Hyatt Regency Lost Pines lights its Christmas tree at 6 p.m. in the lobby, and the event is open to the public. Santa and Mrs. Claus are scheduled to arrive for the lighting.

In town, Bastrop's annual tree lighting at Fisherman's Park and the opening of the River of Lights are also Friday evening. Times and details are in our Thanksgiving week guide: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-week-2026-tree-lighting-wassail-fest

**Also Friday**

The Colorado River Run, with trail races from 5K up to 250K, is scheduled at McKinney Roughs Nature Park.
