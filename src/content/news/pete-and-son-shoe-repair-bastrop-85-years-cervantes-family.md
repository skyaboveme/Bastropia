On a holiday weekend built around shopping local, here is one of downtown Bastrop's longest-running family businesses.

Pete & Son Shoe Repair, at **910 Water St.**, has been fixing boots and shoes for Bastrop customers for about **85 years**, Community Impact reported this week.

**How it started**

Gilbert Cervantes told Community Impact that his father, Pete, learned to make shoes at age 12 from relatives in León, Mexico. Political unrest disrupted the family's business there, and Pete moved to the United States.

He began repairing shoes for Bastrop customers in **1941**. Downtown was busy then with soldiers from nearby Camp Swift, and Gilbert said his father remembered soldiers paying extra to have their shoes and boots fixed first.

**A son comes home**

Gilbert remembers watching his father nail heels onto shoes when he was 7. When Pete retired in **1981**, Gilbert left a firefighting career in North Texas to take over the shop. He told Community Impact he also wanted his children to grow up in a small town.

Today he and his wife, **Mary**, run the shop. Gilbert still works with his father's hammer and the shop's original machines.

Many customers remember coming in as children with their parents or grandparents, Gilbert said, and the couple enjoys hearing their memories of his father. Newer residents are often glad to learn a boot and shoe repair shop is still in town.

**What they do**

According to Community Impact, the shop offers:

- Shoe and boot restoration
- Cleaning and polishing
- Leather purse and belt repairs
- Boot stretching

**Plan your visit**

The Cervantes family has cut back its hours as the couple eases into retirement. The shop is open **Tuesdays through Thursdays, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.** Holiday weeks can change schedules, so call ahead at **512-321-6736**.
