The Elgin ISD board met Thursday and left Superintendent Christopher Bailey’s status unresolved after a recent driving-under-the-influence charge.

The Elgin Courier reports trustees delayed a decision on what comes next. Bailey remains the named subject of the charge; the board has not published a final personnel action from that meeting.

School status for students is separate from that personnel question. Watch the district for any further board notice.
