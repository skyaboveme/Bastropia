Volunteers in Elgin are getting ready to feed thousands of neighbors on Thanksgiving morning, and they're asking for help.

Operation Turkey's Elgin team plans to prepare and deliver **3,200 meals** on Thursday, Nov. 26, the Elgin Courier reported Nov. 19. This year the group is adding **Bastrop and Coupland** to its delivery area.

**A fast-growing local effort**

According to the Courier, the Elgin chapter served about 1,100 meals its first year and 2,590 in 2024. Last Thanksgiving, more than 3,000 meals went out with help from an estimated 350 volunteers, who delivered across Elgin, McDade, Paige, Giddings and Lexington.

Shametra McGregor, who leads the Elgin team, told the Courier that the group tries to reach people who are easy to miss, including veterans, seniors, people experiencing homelessness and those who can't leave home. Operation Turkey started in Austin, runs entirely on volunteers and has no paid staff.

**Ways to help**

Operation Turkey's Elgin page lists these dates:

- **Wednesday, Nov. 25, 8 a.m.–8 p.m.:** Turkey Tailgate at Dave & Buster's, 9333 Research Blvd., Austin, where volunteers smoke turkeys and collect donations for care packages. Smokers and pitmasters are a big need.
- **Wednesday, Nov. 25, 6:30–7:30 p.m.:** Volunteer orientation at Morelia Mexican Grill, 608 W. Alamo St., Elgin.
- **Thursday, Nov. 26, 7 a.m.–noon:** Meals are prepared, packaged and delivered from Morelia Mexican Grill in Elgin.

The event listing says delivery drivers are especially needed. Volunteers should dress for partly outdoor work, wear closed-toe shoes and expect to get a little messy. Register under the Elgin location at https://www.operationturkey.com/locations/elgin/ or through the Eventbrite listing.

**Know someone who could use a meal?**

Organizers are encouraging residents to help connect neighbors with the service. The Elgin team can be reached at ElginTX@operationturkey.com.
