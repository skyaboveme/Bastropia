Good morning, Bastrop County. The National Weather Service expects a partly sunny, breezy Tuesday with a high near 54, north wind gusts up to 30 mph and a 20% chance of showers after 3 p.m. Tonight drops to about 33. Wednesday night could reach 31, a light freeze, so plan to bring pets and tender plants inside. Thanksgiving looks sunny, near 59. There were no weather alerts early this morning.

**Today's stories**

- **Tax bills are in the mail:** About 75,660 bills went out this week. Pay by Feb. 1, 2027, to avoid penalties; there's a new payment website. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-2026-property-tax-bills-mailed-due-feb-1
- **Elgin's Dec. 1 agenda:** A $3.11 million city purchase from the EDC, a business park sale tied to about 30 jobs and an $817,000 water plant change order. https://bastropia.com/news/elgin-council-dec-1-edc-land-deals-water-plant-change-order
- **Double Eagle Ranch PID:** Buyers of 196 new homes would pay a yearly assessment, about $2,984 by one estimate. Hearing Dec. 14. https://bastropia.com/news/double-eagle-ranch-pid-196-homes-assessments-dec-14-hearing
- **Cold nights ahead:** A light freeze is possible Wednesday night, then a sunny Thanksgiving. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-cold-nights-light-freeze-possible-thanksgiving-week

**Coming up**

- **Wednesday, Nov. 25:** Calvary Episcopal serves its 39th Thanksgiving Eve dinner from 4 to 6 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary-2026-11-25
- **Friday, Nov. 27:** Bastrop's annual tree lighting at Fisherman's Park starts at 6 p.m., and the River of Lights opens for the season. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-s-annual-tree-lighting-2026-2026-11-27

**Church and community**

The Pentecostals of Bastrop, 261 FM 20, posted Sunday's message, "My God Shall Supply," on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0HPSjdWqZkw. Sunday worship streams live at 11 a.m. In Elgin, St. Peter's Lutheran Church plans its live nativity in Veterans Memorial Park on Saturday, Dec. 5, with the scene staying up through Dec. 27, according to the city's Dec. 1 council packet.

**At the county and city halls**

Bastrop County Commissioners Court has public hearings set for Monday, Dec. 14, including the Double Eagle Ranch assessments and a Mesa Vista replat, and a Dec. 28 hearing on a Hidden Oaks replat. No new county agenda had posted early Tuesday. In Elgin, the Historic Review Board meets Wednesday at 7:30 a.m.; the EDC board meets Dec. 1 at 5 p.m., City Council at 6:30 p.m., and the Parks & Recreation Advisory Board Dec. 2 at 5:30 p.m. Agendas: https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/.
