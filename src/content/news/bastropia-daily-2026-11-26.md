Happy Thanksgiving, Bastrop County. It's a quiet news day for the holiday, so today's edition focuses on the weather and the long weekend ahead.

The National Weather Service has Bastrop County under a **freeze warning until 9 a.m.**, with lows as low as 31. After that, expect a sunny Thanksgiving with a high near 58. Tonight drops to about 33 again, then highs reach about 66 Friday and 73 Saturday.

**Today's stories**

- **Holiday weekend forecast:** A freezing start, a warm and windy Saturday, and a dry, gusty front Sunday. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-thanksgiving-forecast-freeze-warning-warm-windy-weekend
- **Tree lighting Friday:** Bastrop lights its tree at Fisherman's Park, 6 to 8 p.m., and River of Lights opens for the season. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-tree-lighting-river-of-lights-friday-nov-27-2026
- **An 85-year family shop:** Gilbert and Mary Cervantes keep Pete & Son Shoe Repair going on Water Street, using his father's tools. https://bastropia.com/news/pete-and-son-shoe-repair-bastrop-85-years-cervantes-family
- **This weekend:** Rockne Market Day, a new holiday exhibit at the county museum and the first Sunday of Advent. https://bastropia.com/news/thanksgiving-weekend-2026-bastrop-county-rockne-market-day-museum-holiday-exhibit

**Coming up**

- **Friday, Nov. 27:** Bastrop's annual tree lighting, Fisherman's Park, 6 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-s-annual-tree-lighting-2026-2026-11-27
- **Saturday, Nov. 28:** Rockne Market Day, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., 4045 FM 535. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/rockne-market-day-2026-2026-11-28

**Church and community**

Sunday is the first Sunday of Advent. The Pentecostals of Bastrop, 261 FM 20, worship at 11 a.m. and livestream the service; recent messages are on the church's YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCsh-GCAlLiVJs7E8Ags1UDg. In Elgin, St. Peter's Lutheran Church plans a live nativity in Veterans Memorial Park on Saturday, Dec. 5.

**At the county and city halls**

County and city offices are closed today and Friday. No new Commissioners Court agenda had posted early Thursday; public hearings are already set for Monday, Dec. 14, including the Double Eagle Ranch assessments and a Mesa Vista replat. The court meets at 804 Pecan St., Bastrop. In Elgin, the EDC board meets Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 5 p.m. and City Council at 6:30 p.m., and the Library Advisory Board meets Dec. 3 at 5:30 p.m. Agendas: https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/.
