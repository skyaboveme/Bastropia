Commissioner David Glass won another term in Precinct 4 on Nov. 3. Unofficial returns reported by the Elgin Courier gave Glass about 74.4% against 25.6% for a write-in candidate.

The seat stays with Glass unless the canvass changes the count. See [Results](/results) for the county board.
