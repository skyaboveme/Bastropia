The Nov. 9 regular term of Bastrop County Commissioners Court is past. On the county’s fiscal year 2026–2027 order, the next regular term is Monday, November 23, at the courthouse annex, 804 Pecan Street, in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor. After that, the order lists December 14 and December 28.

Regular terms fall on the second and fourth Mondays unless a county holiday moves the day. The order sets the day and the room. It does not set the hour. That is on the agenda the county posts before each meeting.
