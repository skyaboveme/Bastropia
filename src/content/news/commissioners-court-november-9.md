The next regular term of Bastrop County Commissioners Court is Monday, November 9, at the courthouse annex, 804 Pecan Street. The county’s fiscal year 2026–2027 order sets regular terms on the second and fourth Mondays, in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor, unless a county holiday moves the day. Tuesday, October 13 was the holiday exception. Monday, October 26 was the last regular term. After November 9, the order lists November 23, December 14, and December 28.

The order sets the day and the room. It does not set the hour. That is on the agenda, which the county posts on its website.
