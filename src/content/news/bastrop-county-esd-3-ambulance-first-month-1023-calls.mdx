**The first-month numbers**

Bastrop County Emergency Services District No. 3 began running its own ambulance service on **Oct. 1**. Executive Director Allen Johnson told the Commissioners Court the district had logged:

- **1,023 incidents**
- **1,334 patient contacts**
- **727 hospital transports**
- **15 cardiac arrests** and **15 air medical flights** in October

Johnson also pointed to two patients who survived cardiac arrests after care from first responders, the Courier reported.

**What changed**

Voters created ESD No. 3 in November 2024 to pay for and improve EMS as the county grows. Elgin voters chose to join the district in November 2025. The district has since moved from a contract with Acadian Ambulance to its own system.

It now runs **eight 24-hour ambulances** and **three 12-hour ambulances**, with two supervisors. For the first time, there are ambulance stations in **Red Rock, KC Estates and along FM 1704**.

**Shorter trips, more ambulances available**

About half of the patients the district transported stayed at local medical facilities instead of going to Austin-area hospitals, according to the report. Johnson estimated that saved close to 600 hours of ambulance time, about 90 minutes for each of those transports, so crews could stay in the county for the next call.

**Still being worked on**

Johnson said communication between EMS and fire dispatch systems still needs work, and the district is working to improve information sharing to cut delays. Commissioner David Glass praised the change, telling the court the county hadn't had this level of care before.

**What it means for you**

In an emergency, call 911 as always. Nothing changes in how you request an ambulance.
