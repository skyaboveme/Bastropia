**The game that earned it**

The Statesman cited Nielsen's performance in the Bears' Class 5A Division II bi-district win over Victoria West. According to the [Statesman](https://www.statesman.com/sports/high-school/football/article/uil-high-school-football-playoffs-22532742.php), he completed 26 of 32 passes for 304 yards and six touchdowns and also ran for a touchdown.

Receiver Dallas Crescenzo also made the Statesman's list of standout players for the week, with eight catches for 79 yards and two touchdowns.

*A note on the numbers:* Different outlets have reported slightly different figures for that game. The Statesman lists a 63–43 final, while the MaxPreps recap we used on Nov. 15 had 62–43 and lower passing yardage. Either way, the Bears are moving on.

**Next up: Huntsville**

Bastrop plays Huntsville in the area round on **Friday, Nov. 20, at 7 p.m. at Rhodes Stadium in Katy**, according to published playoff schedules. The winner advances to the regional quarterfinals.

- Check Bastrop ISD and Bastrop High School athletics channels for ticket and streaming information.
- Katy is a long drive. Leave early and plan for Friday-evening traffic around Houston.

Full game details are in our earlier preview: https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-bears-huntsville-area-round-katy-nov-20

Congratulations, Weston, and good luck Friday, Bears!
