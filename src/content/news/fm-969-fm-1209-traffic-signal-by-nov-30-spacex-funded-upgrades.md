Drivers who use the intersection of FM 969 and FM 1209 in Bastrop County should soon see a working traffic signal instead of a flashing beacon.

Texas Department of Transportation spokesperson Antonio Lujan told Community Impact that signal installation started in October and is expected to be finished **no later than Nov. 30**.

**What's changing**

According to TxDOT, the project includes:

- A northbound right-turn lane on FM 1209.
- An eastbound acceleration lane on FM 969.
- Converting the flashing beacon to a full traffic signal.

Crews began putting in utilities and road signs in mid-November, and more paving is expected next, Lujan said. TxDOT has not set a date for finishing the whole project.

**Who is paying**

The work is estimated at **$1.12 million**, covering design, construction and state oversight. SpaceX is paying for it, according to Community Impact.

The intersection is a busy one. Development has continued along both roads, including expansions of Elon Musk's companies in Bastrop County.

**How we got here**

This is the second round of work at the intersection. A separate **$2 million** project that began in July 2024 realigned FM 1209 so it meets FM 969 at a T.

**What drivers should do**

- Expect lane shifts and crews while paving continues.
- Once the signal is switched on, treat it like any new signal: slow down on your approach and watch for drivers who are used to the old beacon.
- Check DriveTexas.org for current lane closures before you head out.
