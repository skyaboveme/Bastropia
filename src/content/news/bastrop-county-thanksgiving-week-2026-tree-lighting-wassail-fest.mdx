Thanksgiving week is a short one, and the holiday season gets going right after the turkey. Here's what's on the Bastrop County Calendar for Nov. 23–29. Times can change, so check the listing or the organizer before you go.

<StatBox value="Nov. 27" label="Bastrop's annual tree lighting at Fisherman's Park and the start of River of Lights, which runs through Jan. 4" source="Bastrop County Calendar" sourceUrl="https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-tx-river-of-lights-2026-2026-11-27" />

**Monday, Nov. 23**

- **Bastrop County Commissioners Court**, 9 a.m., 804 Pecan St., Bastrop. On the agenda: the 2026 tax levy and a discussion of possible tax breaks for EdgeConneX. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-edgeconnex-tax-break-talk-state-data-center-exemption
- **Elgin Prime Timers Friendsgiving** field trip to Headlong Farm, 2 to 4 p.m., a City of Elgin Parks & Recreation outing. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/prime-timer-field-trip-friendsgiving-at-headlong-farm-2026-11-23

**Wednesday, Nov. 25 (Thanksgiving Eve)**

- **Calvary Episcopal Church's 39th Thanksgiving Eve Dinner**, 4 to 6 p.m. in the Parish Hall, 1028 Main St., Bastrop. The dinner is especially for people who are homebound, have special needs or are spending the holiday alone. The church lists local home delivery from noon to 5 p.m. and is asking for volunteers and dishes (512-303-7515). https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary-2026-11-25
- **Senior Game Day** at the Elgin library, noon to 3 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/senior-game-day-2026-11-25

**Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving)**

Expect closures. The Bastrop Chamber of Commerce office will be closed, and the City of Elgin has its holiday schedule posted on its calendar.

**Friday, Nov. 27**

- **Bastrop's annual tree lighting** at Fisherman's Park, listed for 6 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-s-annual-tree-lighting-2026-2026-11-27
- **River of Lights** opens on the half-mile June Hill Pape Riverwalk and runs through Jan. 4. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/bastrop-tx-river-of-lights-2026-2026-11-27

**Saturday, Nov. 28 (Small Business Saturday)**

- **Smithville Turkey Trot**, 9 a.m., starting at the Smithville Recreation Center. Run, jog or walk a 5K or 1 mile. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/smithville-turkey-trot-2026-2026-11-28
- **Wassail Fest**, 4 to 8 p.m. in downtown Bastrop, the yearly Small Business Saturday stroll. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/wassail-fest-2026-2026-11-28
- **Shop Small Saturday** in Elgin, on the city's events calendar from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/shop-small-saturday-2026-11-28
- **Holiday Traditions from Around the World** opens at the Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center, 904 Main St., and runs through Dec. 31. It looks at how different cultures and faiths celebrate Christmas, Hanukkah, Kwanzaa and more. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/holiday-traditions-from-around-the-world-2026-11-28

**Sunday, Nov. 29**

It's the first Sunday of Advent. Bastrop Bible Church and First Baptist Church of Elgin both list it on their calendars.

**Coming up**

Bastrop's restored Old Iron Bridge reopens with a celebration on Thursday, Dec. 3. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-old-iron-bridge-reopens-dec-3

See the full week at https://bastropcountycalendar.com.
