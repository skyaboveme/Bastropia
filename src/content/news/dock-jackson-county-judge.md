Dock Jackson unseated County Judge Gregory Klaus on Nov. 3, according to unofficial results. The Elgin Courier’s tally had Jackson at 51.5% and Klaus at 48.5%.

That margin can still move slightly before the canvass. Race-by-race numbers and other county contests are on [Results](/results).
