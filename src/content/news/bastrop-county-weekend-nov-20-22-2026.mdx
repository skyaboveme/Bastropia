The weather looks pleasant for getting out: after some morning fog, the National Weather Service expects highs in the mid- to upper 70s Thursday and Friday. Here's what's on the calendar around Bastrop County. Check each listing before you go, since details can change.

**Thursday, Nov. 19**

- **McDade Colonial Supper, 6 p.m.** The McDade Historical Society is hosting a Colonial Supper at the McDade Historical Museum to mark the nation's 250th anniversary, according to the Bastrop County Calendar listing. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/mcdade-s-colonial-supper-2026-2026-11-19

**Friday, Nov. 20**

- **Chili Cook-Off, Late Night at The Bodega, 6–11 p.m.** at Hyperloop Plaza, 865 FM 1209, Bastrop. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/chili-cook-off-late-night-at-the-bodega-2026-11-20

**Saturday, Nov. 21**

- **Trot with Tracy's 5K for CASA, 8 a.m.** The race starts promptly at 8 a.m. on a route through historic downtown Bastrop, and runners have until 10 a.m. to finish, according to Discover Bastrop. Afterward, local food and beverage vendors will set up at Fisherman's Park for an after-party and the raffle drawing. Proceeds support CASA of Bastrop, Fayette and Lee Counties, whose trained volunteers advocate in court for children who have experienced abuse or neglect. Register, sponsor, buy raffle tickets or donate: https://runsignup.com/Race/TX/Bastrop/TrotWithTracySForCASA
- **Family Nature Quest, 10:30 a.m.** at McKinney Roughs Nature Park, with nature-play activities for parents and kids, per the Bastrop County Calendar. https://bastropcountycalendar.com/
- **Bastrop CATS Kitty BINGO: Rhinestone Rodeo, 5:15 p.m.** (VIP 4:30 p.m.) at the Bastrop Convention & Exhibit Center, 1408 Chestnut St. Dinner, bingo, raffles and a live auction to help local community cats. Read our full preview: https://bastropia.com/news/kitty-bingo-bastrop-cats-nov-21-2026
- **Live music: Jaime Krueger Group, 7:30–10:30 p.m.** at The Grace Miller ("Gracie's"). https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/jaime-krueger-group-2026-11-21

**Sunday, Nov. 22**

- **Hanging of the Greens, 3–5 p.m.** at Bastrop Bible Church, 602 Pecan St., Bastrop. The church is decorating for Advent and Christmas, with refreshments; guests are asked to bring a treat to share. https://bastropbible.churchcenter.com/calendar/event/242607548

**Looking ahead**

- **Monday, Nov. 23, 9 a.m.:** Bastrop County Commissioners Court regular session, 804 Pecan St., Bastrop. Agenda: https://bastropco.civicweb.net/document/128202
- **Wednesday, Nov. 25, 4–6 p.m.:** Calvary Episcopal Church's 39th annual Thanksgiving Eve Dinner at the Parish Hall, 1028 Main St., Bastrop, with local home delivery from noon to 5 p.m. https://cecbastrop.org/seniors/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary/
