Happy Friday after Thanksgiving, Bastrop County. County and city offices are closed today, the weather has turned milder, and the holiday weekend is just getting started.

The National Weather Service calls for a **sunny** day with a high near **68** and a mostly clear night around **43**. Saturday warms to about 74, then a dry, gusty front arrives Sunday.

**Today's stories**

- **Weekend forecast:** A sunny Friday for the tree lighting, a breezy Saturday, and Sunday gusts that may approach wind-advisory strength. Rain chances return late next week. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-friday-forecast-sunny-tree-lighting-gusty-sunday
- **Smithville Turkey Trot:** The annual 5K or 1-mile run-or-walk is 9 to 11:30 a.m. Saturday at the Recreation Center. State parks also list midmorning programs. https://bastropia.com/news/smithville-turkey-trot-saturday-park-programs-2026
- **Help with benefits:** St. David's Foundation is providing $1.58 million to three organizations that help Bastrop County residents enroll in SNAP, Medicaid and other programs. https://bastropia.com/news/st-davids-foundation-1-58-million-bastrop-county-benefits-navigation

**Tonight**

- **Rivals for a Cause**, 1 to 10 p.m. on Main Street in downtown Bastrop, with the Texas A&M-Texas game at 6:30 p.m. https://bastropia.com/news/black-friday-2026-bastrop-rivals-for-a-cause-optoutside-hike
- **Bastrop's tree lighting**, 6 to 8 p.m. at Fisherman's Park. River of Lights opens the same night, 6 to 10 p.m. through Jan. 4. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-tree-lighting-river-of-lights-friday-nov-27-2026

**Coming up Saturday**

- **Smithville Turkey Trot**, 9 to 11:30 a.m., Recreation Center, 106 Gazley St.
- **Rockne Market Day**, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., 4045 FM 535. https://bastropia.com/news/thanksgiving-weekend-2026-bastrop-county-rockne-market-day-museum-holiday-exhibit
- **Shop Small Saturday** in downtown Elgin, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and **Lost Pines Wassail Fest** in downtown Bastrop, 3 to 7 p.m. https://bastropia.com/news/small-business-saturday-2026-wassail-fest-bastrop-shop-small-elgin

**Church and community**

Sunday is the first Sunday of Advent. The Pentecostals of Bastrop, 261 FM 20, worship at 11 a.m. and livestream the service. More worship listings: https://bastropia.com/news/thanksgiving-weekend-2026-bastrop-county-rockne-market-day-museum-holiday-exhibit

**At the county and city halls**

County and city offices are closed today. No new Commissioners Court agenda had posted Friday morning; the next regular meeting is Monday, Dec. 14, at 804 Pecan St., Bastrop, with public hearings already set on the calendar. In Elgin, the EDC board meets Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 5 p.m. and City Council at 6:30 p.m., and the Library Advisory Board meets Dec. 3 at 5:30 p.m. Agendas: https://elgintx.portal.civicclerk.com/.
