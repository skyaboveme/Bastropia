Thanksgiving is Thursday, and most local government offices in Bastrop County will be closed for two days. Here's what to know before you run errands or roll the trash cart out.

**County offices**

Bastrop County offices are closed **Thursday, Nov. 26, and Friday, Nov. 27**, according to the county's posted holiday schedule. That includes the courthouse offices at 804 Pecan St. Plan any tax office, clerk or elections business for today or next week.

**City of Bastrop**

City offices are closed Nov. 26 and 27, according to the city's holiday list.

Texas Disposal Systems (TDS), the city's trash and recycling hauler, observes Thanksgiving. Its service rule is that pickup slides **one day** after the normal collection day, so a Thursday route should expect Friday service and a Friday route Saturday service. The TDS Waste Wizard app shows your exact day. Questions go to Bastrop Utility Customer Service at **512-332-8830**.

The Bastrop Public Library closes at **5 p.m. today**, stays closed Thursday through Saturday, Nov. 26–28, and reopens Monday, Nov. 30, at 10 a.m.

**Elgin**

Elgin city offices are closed Nov. 26 and 27, according to the city calendar. The city's 2026 trash calendar marks Thanksgiving as a trash holiday, when service slides one day. Utility Billing can answer questions at 512-281-5724, option 2.

**Smithville**

Smithville city offices close at **noon today** and stay closed Thursday and Friday, the city says. Trash and recycling in Smithville are picked up on Wednesdays, according to TDS, so today's pickup should run as usual.

**Outside city limits**

Rural residents with a private hauler should check with that company, since holiday schedules vary.

**Need a meal or a place to gather?**

Calvary Episcopal Church serves its 39th Thanksgiving Eve dinner today from 4 to 6 p.m. Details are on the Bastrop County Calendar: https://bastropcountycalendar.com/events/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary-2026-11-25

Have a safe and happy Thanksgiving, Bastrop County.
