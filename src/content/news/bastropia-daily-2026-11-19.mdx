Good morning, Bastrop County. Take it slow on the early commute: the National Weather Service issued a Special Weather Statement before dawn for patchy dense fog from the Coastal Prairies into parts of the I-35 corridor, with visibility down to a quarter mile at Giddings around 3 a.m. Leave extra space and use low-beam headlights. Once the fog lifts, expect a mostly cloudy Thursday with a high near 75, a mostly clear night near 52, and a sunny Friday with a high near 78.

**Today's stories**

- **Elgin's mixed-drink vote is official:** The City Council canvassed the Nov. 3 election, which passed with 77.6% of 3,347 ballots, so alcohol sales including mixed drinks are now allowed across current city limits. Council also swore in Ward 1's Jessica Jaimes. https://bastropia.com/news/elgin-voters-approve-citywide-mixed-drink-sales
- **Courthouse roof work resumes:** A bee infestation, scheduling and weather pushed the roof deadline at the historic courthouse to Dec. 4. The separate $23.4 million restoration could run until 2028–2032. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-courthouse-roof-repairs-resume-bee-infestation
- **This weekend:** A CASA 5K downtown, a chili cook-off, family nature play at McKinney Roughs, live music and more. https://bastropia.com/news/bastrop-county-weekend-nov-20-22-2026
- **Kitty BINGO returns Saturday:** Bastrop CATS' 11th annual fundraiser helps the group that has fixed and vaccinated more than 12,000 local cats. https://bastropia.com/news/kitty-bingo-bastrop-cats-nov-21-2026
