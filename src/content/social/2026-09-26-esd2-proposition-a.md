Bastrop County Emergency Services District No. 2 posted that Proposition A on the November ballot would add a 348.1-acre tract to the district. The page says an annexation has to pass with voters in the current district and in the property being added. It says an earlier vote passed in the current district and failed overall because no registered voter in the annexation area cast a ballot.
