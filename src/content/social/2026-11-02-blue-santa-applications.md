The Bastrop Police Department is accepting applications for the 2026 Blue Santa program from November 2 through 11:59 pm on November 20. The department says to read the eligibility rules on the flyer before applying.

The form is on the city's community services page. Completed applications can be emailed to BPDBlueSanta@cityofbastrop.org or brought to the police station at 104 Grady Tuck Lane during business hours. The City of Bastrop says applications are also available at City Hall.
