Community Impact lists the Red, White & Blue Veterans of Bastrop County Banquet for Friday, November 14, 2026, from 6 pm to 9 pm at the Bastrop Convention & Exhibit Center, 1408 Chestnut Street.

The evening includes a catered dinner, raffles, and live music. Ticket prices on the listing are $30 for veterans and spouses and $40 for other adults. Proceeds support the Bastrop County Veterans Honor Guard, Bastrop and Elgin High School NJROTC, Restoration Ranch, and Bastrop Memorial and Veterans Day ceremonies. Tickets are through [rwbveterans.com](http://www.rwbveterans.com).
