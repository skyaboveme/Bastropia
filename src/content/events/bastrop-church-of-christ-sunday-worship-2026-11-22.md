Bastrop Church of Christ lists Sunday Bible study classes at 9:30 a.m. in the Angel Annex and a worship service at 10:30 a.m. The church says the service lasts about 75 minutes and includes music and a message. The building is at 287 FM 20 in Bastrop.

The church also lists home fellowships at 5:30 p.m. every other Sunday. This listing does not assume November 22 is one of those Sundays. Confirm the day on [bastropcoc.org](https://www.bastropcoc.org/visit-with-us/) before you go.
