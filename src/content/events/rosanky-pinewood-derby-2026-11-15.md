Rosanky Baptist Church is hosting a Community Pinewood Derby Race on Sunday, November 15, at 5 pm at 2160 FM 535 in Rosanky. Trophies are awarded for fastest car and most creative car. Free food and refreshments are part of the evening.

Bring your family and your car, or come to cheer, eat, and visit with neighbors. The church says everyone in the community is welcome. Call (512) 360-3968 or see [rosankybc.org](https://www.rosankybc.org/) for details.
