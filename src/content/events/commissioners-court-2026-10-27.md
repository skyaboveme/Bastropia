The county’s meeting order sets a regular Commissioners Court term for Monday, October 27, 2026. Court meets in the Commissioners Courtroom on the second floor of the courthouse annex at 804 Pecan Street.

The order names the day, not the hour. Read the agenda on the [county website](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/) for the start time and the items on the docket.
