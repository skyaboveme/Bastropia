Cedar Creek United Methodist Church lists Sunday worship at 10:00 a.m. in the sanctuary. The church says the service mixes hymns and praise songs, scripture, and preaching. Holy Communion is on the first Sunday of the month, so November 22 is not that Sunday. The nursery is listed from 9:45 to 11:05 a.m. for children from 2 months through 4 years. Sunday school is listed 15 minutes after worship ends.

The church is at 5630 FM 535 in Cedar Creek, at the intersection of FM 535 and Highway 21. The published phone number is (512) 303-1393. Confirm the day on [cedarcreekumc.org](https://www.cedarcreekumc.org/services-schedule) before you go.
