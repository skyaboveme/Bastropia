The Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center lists Bastrop Art Fest at the Museum for Saturday, November 7, 2026, from 10 am to 5 pm at 904 Main St. As part of the Lost Pines Art Center's Bastrop Art Fest, the museum is showing rarely seen artwork from its collection for one day only.

The museum says the viewing is included with museum admission. Call (512) 303-0904 with questions.
