The Bastrop County Historical Society lists its Historic Homes Tour for Saturday, December 12, 2026, from noon to 5 pm. Visitors can tour five historic homes and one historic church, each decorated for the holidays, with local volunteer docents sharing the stories behind the buildings.

Tickets are $25 on the museum's site, and children 3 and under get a free ticket. Check the listing for the six stops.
