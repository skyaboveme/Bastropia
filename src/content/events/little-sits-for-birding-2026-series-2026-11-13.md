Attending the Little Hike series with bird watching in Bastrop County, Texas, is a peaceful way to start your day in nature on the second Friday of each month. Hosted by Bird City Bastrop and local birding partners, these easy, guided morning hikes happen at different parks and trails around the county, where expert birders help you spot and identify native and migratory birds while you walk. You’ll enjoy gentle scenery, learn birding basics, and can borrow binoculars if you don’t have your own.
