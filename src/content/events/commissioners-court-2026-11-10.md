Monday, November 10, 2026 is a regular Commissioners Court day under the county’s fiscal-year meeting order. Court meets in the annex courtroom at 804 Pecan Street in Bastrop.

The start time is on the agenda, not in the order. Look it up on the [county website](https://www.bastropcounty.gov/) the week of the meeting.
