On the second Saturday of November, Smithville hangs handmade quilts through downtown and pairs the day with the garden club home tour. In 2026 that Saturday is November 14, about 9 am to 4 pm.

The Smithville Stitchers Quilt Guild lists a donation quilt drawing, bed turning, opportunity baskets, a gift boutique, refreshments, and local vendors. The garden club invites visitors into historic homes the same day. Confirm hours and tour tickets with the hosts before you go.
