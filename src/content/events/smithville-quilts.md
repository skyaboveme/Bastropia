On the second Saturday of November, Smithville hangs handmade quilts through downtown and pairs the day with the garden club home tour. In 2026 that Saturday is November 14.

This desk is using the traditional date. Hours, the tour tickets, and any change of plans belong to the city and the garden club. Check with them before you go.
