The Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center lists the Caroling Tour on Main for Saturday, December 19, 2026, from 5:30 pm to 6:30 pm, starting from the museum at 904 Main St. The walking tour goes down Main Street with holiday carols and stories about downtown Bastrop's historic buildings.

The museum says tickets will go on sale soon online and in person at the museum, and every ticket supports the Bastrop County Historical Society. It has not posted a price yet.
