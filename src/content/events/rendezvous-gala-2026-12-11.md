The Bastrop County Historical Society lists its Rendezvous Gala for Friday, December 11, 2026, from 6 pm to 10 pm at the Bastrop Convention & Exhibit Center, 1408 Chestnut St B. The evening has a vintage holiday theme, with seasonal drinks and bites, a silent auction, and a raffle, and it supports preserving and promoting Bastrop County history.

The listing sells a Rendezvous Ticket for $100, plus sponsor levels from $300 to $2,000. Tickets are reserved on the source page.
