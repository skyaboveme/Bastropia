First Presbyterian Church of Elgin lists in-person worship every Sunday at 10:00 a.m. in the sanctuary. The church says worship usually lasts 55 to 60 minutes, and that Communion is on the second and fourth Sundays of the month. November 22 is the fourth Sunday. The church is at 303 N. Avenue C in Elgin.

A live feed of the same service is listed on the church’s Facebook page at 10:00 a.m. Confirm the day on [fpcelgin.org](https://www.fpcelgin.org/) before you go.
