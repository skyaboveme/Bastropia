Admission and parking are free, making it easy for families, friends and visitors to spend the day exploring the vineyard and enjoying the changing season.  Guests can shop for handmade goods, crafts and holiday gifts from local artisans while sipping award-winning wine and enjoying a variety of food and beverage option
