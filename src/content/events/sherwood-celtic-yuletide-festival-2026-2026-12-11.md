Sherwood Celtic Yuletide Festival returns Dec.11–13, 2026, at Sherwood Forest Faire in McDade, Texas, for a three-day celebration of Celtic music, culture, and holiday traditions. The festival features more than 30 live bands and 90 artisans, along with Scottish Highland Games, Celtic archery, holiday shopping, mead, and visits with Santa.
