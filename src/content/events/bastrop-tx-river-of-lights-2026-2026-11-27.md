Experience the magic of the season along Bastrop, Texas’ beautiful River of Lights during the holiday season! Stroll the half-mile June Hill Pape Riverwalk, transformed into a twinkling wonderland with hundreds of sparkling displays in festive holiday themes. Enjoy the sights and sounds of the season as the trail winds along the scenic Colorado River.  Access the Riverwalk from the stairs west of Main and Pine Streets or use the fully accessible entrance at Fisherman’s Park on Willow Street.
