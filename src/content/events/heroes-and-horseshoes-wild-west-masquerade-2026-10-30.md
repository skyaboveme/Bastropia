Restoration Ranch lists its Wild West Masquerade for Friday, October 30, 2026, from 6 pm to 10 pm at the Bastrop Convention Center. Guests are asked to wear Western attire and a masquerade mask.

The evening has live music from the Nathan Colt Young Band, auction items, raffles, heavy appetizers, and a cash bar. Proceeds benefit Restoration Ranch's equine learning and peer support programs, which are free for veterans. Early-bird pricing is $75 per person or $500 for a table of eight.
