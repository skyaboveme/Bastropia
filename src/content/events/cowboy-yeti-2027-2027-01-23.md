The Cowboy YETI returns to Bastrop State Park in Bastrop, Texas, on Jan. 23, 2027, with trail races ranging from a 7K to a challenging 100K. Runners can choose from 7K, 20K, 40K, 60K and 100K distances, or take on the 20K Ruck with a 25-pound minimum pack. The courses wind through Bastrop State Park’s scenic pine forest on dirt and rocky single-track trails, with about 800 feet of elevation gain per 20K loop.
