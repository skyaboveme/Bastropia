On Nov. 19. 2026, the McDade Historical Society will host a Colonial Supper at 6 p.m. in honor of our nation’s 250th anniversary, featuring some of George Washington’s favorite foods – ham, biscuits, macaroni, fruit, apple and cherry pies.  At 7:00, a silent and live auction will be held to raise funds for a new a/c inside the museum.
