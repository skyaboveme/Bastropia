Texmas Trail Race returns Saturday, December 12, 2026, at Buescher State Park in Smithville, Texas, offering trail runners a choice of distances through the scenic Lost Pines. Races include a 104K, 57K, 21-mile, 14-mile, 7-mile, and 1-mile course, along with a four-person relay for the 104K. The trails feature single-track paths through the pine forest, with small climbs, technical sections, and historic landmarks along the way.  Race starts begin early with a 20-hour cutoff.
