Calvary Episcopal Church lists its 39th annual Thanksgiving Eve Dinner for Wednesday, November 25, 2026. Dinner is served from 4 pm to 6 pm. Local home delivery is listed from noon to 5 pm. The page says the meal is for the community, especially people with special needs, people who are homebound, or people spending the holiday alone.

The dinner page does not list a ticket price. The church is still asking for volunteers and dishes at jamie@cecbastrop.org. Confirm the place on the [dinner page](https://cecbastrop.org/seniors/thanksgiving-eve-dinner-at-calvary/) before you go.
