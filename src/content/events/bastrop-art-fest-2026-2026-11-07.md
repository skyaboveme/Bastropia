Bastrop Art Fest, happening Nov. 7, 2026, is the Lost Pines Art Center’s annual event, bringing together artists, makers, community and visitors from all around for a celebration of all things art in Bastrop, Texas. They host diverse and unique vendors selling original artwork, crafts and handmade foods. Other draws for the event are hands-on creative activities and live demonstrations from artists, including blacksmiths and glassblowers.
