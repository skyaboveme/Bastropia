Boo Bash is the downtown Bastrop Halloween gathering on North Main Street and Spring Street. The public listing calls it a free, all-day event for Saturday, October 31.

Costumes are part of the day. Confirm the hours, street closures, and parking with the organizer before you go, especially if you are bringing small children.
