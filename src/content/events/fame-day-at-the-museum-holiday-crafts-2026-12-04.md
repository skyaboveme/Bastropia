The Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center lists FAME Day at the Museum: Holiday Crafts for Friday, December 4, 2026, from 5 pm to 8 pm at 904 Main St. Families and visitors of all ages can stop by the museum porch to make homemade holiday decorations.

The museum says the event is free and open to everyone.
