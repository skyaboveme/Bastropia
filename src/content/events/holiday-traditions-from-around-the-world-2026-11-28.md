The Bastrop County Museum & Visitor Center lists Holiday Traditions from Around the World from November 28, 2026, 10 am, through December 31, 2026, 5 pm, at 904 Main St. The exhibit explores how different cultures and faiths celebrate holidays like Christmas, Hanukkah, and Kwanzaa, and the museum invites visitors to share their own family traditions.

The grand opening is during Wassail Fest on November 28. The museum is open Monday through Saturday 10 am to 5 pm and Sunday 11 am to 3 pm, and it is closed Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and December 26.
