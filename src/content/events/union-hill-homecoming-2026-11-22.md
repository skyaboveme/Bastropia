Union Hill Baptist Church says it will celebrate its 162nd church anniversary and homecoming on Sunday, November 22, at 11:00 a.m. The guest speaker is Rev. Dr. Daryl Horton, pastor of Mt. Zion Baptist Church in Austin. The church says lunch will be served, and it asks members to wear the church color, blue. Friends, family, and former members are invited.

The church is at 1430 FM 969 in Bastrop. The published phone number is (512) 581-3709. Confirm the day on [unionhillbc.org](https://www.unionhillbc.org/) before you go.
