Get ready for a jolly good time at the Bastrop Main Street Program’s annual Merry on Main! Spend the day in Downtown Bastrop, Texas, enjoying festive fun for the whole family. Write letters to Santa, shop ‘til you drop, and soak up the holiday cheer around every corner. With local shops, sweet treats, and plenty of surprises, it’s a day you won’t want to miss!  As the sun sets, continue the celebration with the Lost Pines Christmas Parade, a lighted holiday tradition.
