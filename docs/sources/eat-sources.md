# Eats Beat — restaurant sources

Compiled Monday, Nov. 16, 2026 (America/Chicago), with a deeper Elgin and Smithville pass for Bastropia's Eats Beat column (formerly Eat) on specials and happy hours. Prefer a venue's own website or a current official social post. Flag aggregator listings, undated social posts, and anything older than about six months as "confirm before going." Do not invent deals, times, or prices.

The Nov. 15, 2026, countywide pass remains the baseline for Bastrop and the smaller towns. This update adds every restaurant, bar, brewery, winery, icehouse, BBQ joint, cafe, and fixed food truck found in Elgin and Smithville, including places with no posted specials, so they can be rechecked later.

The PlacesSearch (Google Maps) tool was unavailable during this pass (the Treg connector was down). The inventory was built from the Elgin and Smithville chamber directories, Visit Smithville's dining page, the City of Elgin downtown business guide lead, local press, and many web searches for restaurants, bars, BBQ, Mexican restaurants, breweries, and icehouses in each city.

## How to update this column

1. Start with the venue inventory below and the current `/workspace/bastropia/eat-specials.csv`.
2. Recheck each restaurant's own website, specials or menu page, and recent Facebook or Instagram posts.
3. Recheck chamber and visitor directories for new openings and closures.
4. Keep prices and times only when the venue (or its official location page, for chains) still lists them.
5. Note the date checked. Anything older than about six months, or from an aggregator only, should be marked "confirm before going."
6. Do not reuse specials from a similarly named restaurant in another Texas city (for example, La Cabana Cafe in Lakehills is not the Smithville La Cabana).

## Gaps to remember next time

- The PlacesSearch / Google Maps tool was down, so a map-radius search of every pin in Elgin and Smithville was not possible. Recheck with PlacesSearch when it is available, especially for food trucks and new openings.
- Facebook and Instagram remain the main (sometimes only) specials channel for many independents in both towns. This pass used official websites, chamber and visitor lists, search snippets, and some social-post text that appeared in search results. Full Facebook feeds still could not be reviewed reliably.
- Lightnin' Bar, Morelia, and The Owl post that they have a happy hour, but not the actual deals or, for Morelia and The Owl, the times.
- Swayze's official site names wing-night and beer specials without saying which night.
- Pocket's Grille specials were confirmed for the week of Nov. 16, 2026; the Monday pizza deal also appears as a standing homepage special, but Tuesday–Thursday deals may change week to week.

- TACORREAL's official site was down; recheck Pork Roast Tuesday directly on the site.
- Hope Floats' reopen status should be confirmed in person or on current social posts.
- Store House and Chapala Fiestas notes from the Nov. 15 Bastrop pass still apply.
- Chain restaurants often say "availability varies by location."
- Jalisco's Elgin location has a full bar and changing weekly entertainment; Bastrop and Cedar Creek serve beer and wine only.
