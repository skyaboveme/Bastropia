# Bastropia reference docs (sources)

These files are editorial reference only. They are not wired into the Astro site and should not change site code, layouts, or published pages by themselves. A later change can turn them into public sections such as an Eats Beat page or a church directory page.

Data in this folder was compiled for Bastropia coverage of Bastrop County, Texas. Confirm deals, worship times, and official details against the named source before publishing.

## News and video sources

- `news-and-video-sources.md` — ranked monitoring guide of Bastrop County news outlets, official city and county sites, school districts, emergency/weather sources, and video/meeting platforms (YouTube, Facebook Live, BoardBook, Quorum/Google Drive, and others). Researched and URL-tested November 14, 2026 (America/Chicago). Use this as the daily news and video beat list. It is not a public site section by itself, but it can later support a sources or “where we check” page.

## Eats Beat

Eats Beat is Bastropia's restaurant specials and happy hours column. It covers restaurants, bars, breweries, and similar venues in Bastrop, Elgin, and Smithville.

- Deals in these files were verified November 15–16, 2026 (America/Chicago).
- Confirm every special, happy hour, price, and time with the venue before publishing. Many independents post deals only on Facebook, and listing-site details go stale.
- Interactive working page (filterable by town and day): [Eats Beat: Bastrop County Specials & Happy Hours](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-bc4bda5a-c3be-4ffb-bca9-cd11c69e37e8/2a0e359c-4ec4-4d58-9a97-503579716df3)

Files:

- `eat-specials.csv` — venue rows with city, address, type, happy hour, daily specials, website, source, source date, and certainty (`confirmed`, `confirm`, or similar). Use this as the data source for an Eats Beat page or weekly roundup.
- `eat-sources.md` — monitoring list of venues checked, how to verify deals, and notes on closures and gaps.

## Church directory

- `church-directory.csv` — churches across Bastrop County with denomination, city, address, worship times, website, source, and certainty. Times marked "call/check" or "confirm" were not verified on the church's own site.
- `church-sources.md` — monitoring guide of church websites, calendars, and feeds for daily event coverage.

These can later power a searchable church directory page (filter by town and denomination) and the daily church-events check.

## County government

- `county-government.md` — reporter knowledge base: Commissioners Court, elected offices, departments, budget and tax rate, meeting schedule, and story leads from recent agendas.
- `county-gov-sources.md` — official monitoring URLs (County Clerk agendas and the CivicWeb document archive) and the daily/weekly agenda workflow.

These can later power a county government reference page and automatic agenda-preview stories.
